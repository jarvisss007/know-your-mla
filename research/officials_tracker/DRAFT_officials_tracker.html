After the raid

55 Karnataka officials were raided, trapped, searched or suspended on 10 days between 17 Jan 2026 and 22 Sep 2026, in the reports read so far. This page follows each one from that first day towards a verdict.

A raid or an arrest is an allegation, not a finding of guilt. Asset figures are the agency's first-day estimates. The page gives the post, not the person: names are in the linked reports and are added here only after the FIR and the person's reply are checked.

Each number counts officials whose case reached that step or a later one, as far as the reports read show. A zero means no report of that step was found. It does not mean the step has not happened: the follow-up check on 16 Nov 2026 was a web search by name, mostly in English, and did not cover court case-status sites or Lokayukta records. 7 officials added from a later report have not been checked yet. 6 rows carry a later development of another kind (a court order, further searches, a posting, a separate case); each is shown in the table with its source.

What is missing

  • This is not every case of 2026. No raid report for May, October or November 2026 has been found. A Deccan Herald report dated 20 Aug 2026 on a single Bengaluru official would not open and is not in the table.
  • The follow-up check was a press search. Chargesheets, sanction requests, court stays and verdicts are rarely reported and must come from Lokayukta replies, RTI answers and court records. The outcome of the High Court hearing of 22 Jul 2026 in the excise matter was not found.
  • Two follow-up entries are weak and marked as such: a posting reported by one outlet with the order unseen, and an arrest in a separate case matched by name and former post.
  • Several rows rest on a single news report. Where the district or department is "not stated", the report did not give it.
