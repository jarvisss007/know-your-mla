After the raid

48 Karnataka officials were raided, trapped, searched or suspended on 10 days between 17 Jan 2026 and 22 Sep 2026, in the reports read so far. This page follows each one from that first day towards a verdict.

A raid or an arrest is an allegation, not a finding of guilt. Asset figures are the agency's first-day estimates. The page gives the post, not the person: names are in the linked reports and are added here only after the FIR and the person's reply are checked.
