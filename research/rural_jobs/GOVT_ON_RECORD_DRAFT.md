# MGNREGA in Karnataka: what the government has said on the record (July 2023 – August 2026)

**Method.** I indexed all 101 sitting-day question lists of the 16th Karnataka Assembly (4 Jul 2023 – 27 Aug 2026; 21,655 question rows) and searched titles and question text for NREGA / Udyoga Khatri / social audit / Ombudsman / GP irregularities and for the listed places. I downloaded 33 answers and read 20 of them as page images (they are scans with no text layer). Lok Sabha answers were pulled from sansad.in as text PDFs.

**Conventions.**
- Assembly URL pattern: `https://kla.kar.nic.in/assembly/answers/16assembly/pdf/<DDMMYYYY>/<n>.pdf`, cited below as `DDMMYYYY/n`.
- All Assembly questions cited are unstarred (ಚುಕ್ಕೆ ಗುರುತಿಲ್ಲದ ಪ್ರಶ್ನೆ) and answered by the RDPR Minister, Priyank Kharge, unless noted.
- Amounts are as printed. "≈ crore" conversions are mine.
- Confidence: **High** = clean print, cross-checked by row/column sums where possible. **Medium** = legible scan, not cross-checkable. `[unclear]` = digit not readable.

### 1.3 Action taken (statewide wording)
- **Source:** 19032025/2404 and 19032025/2559 (same text in both).
- Disciplinary action (ಶಿಸ್ತು ಕ್ರಮ) is being taken against government staff responsible.
- Under Rule 7(3)(F) of the MGNREG Audit of Schemes Rules 2011, misappropriated amounts are recovered from outsourced staff. Those in serious financial offences are removed from service and criminal cases (ಕ್ರಿಮಿನಲ್ ಮೊಕದ್ದಮೆ) are filed.
- GP members and presidents face recovery, criminal cases and disqualification under the Karnataka Gram Swaraj and Panchayat Raj Act.
- Circular ಗ್ರಾಅಪ 60 ಜಿಪಅ 2024 dated 05.06.2024 prescribes penalties for PDOs and line-department officers who fail to produce files for social audit.
- **Gap:** no statewide count of suspensions or FIRs is given in either answer.
- **Confidence:** High on wording.

### 2A. KOPPAL

#### Kushtagi: 2019-20 check-dam material bills (three linked answers)

**(i) Assembly 28012026/548, 28-01-2026.** Asked by Doddanagouda Hanumagouda Patil (Kushtagi).
- In 2019-20 the Rural Drinking Water Supply department executed **490 check dams** (ಚೆಕ್ ಡ್ಯಾಂ) in Kushtagi taluk under NREGA.
- After complaints, the State Quality Monitor (SQM) reported that **198 works** were paid more than the work executed.
- A **criminal case was filed against 6 officers**; the matter is before the High Court (ಉಚ್ಚ ನ್ಯಾಯಾಲಯದ ವಿಚಾರಣೆ ಹಂತದಲ್ಲಿ).
- Of 231 works, files of 33 were unavailable. A district/taluk team was told to physically verify the remaining 198. The team reported:
  - 77 works: latitude/longitude (ಅಕ್ಷಾಂಶ ಮತ್ತು ರೇಖಾಂಶ) matched;
  - 60 works: did not match;
  - 16 works: site not shown to the team;
  - 45 works: no report submitted.
- The Executive Engineer was told by letter of 09.12.2024 to fix responsibility and act once records are confirmed correct.
- **Confidence:** High.

**(ii) Assembly 17082026/167, 17-08-2026.** Same MLA. The signature block reads "(ಈಶ್ವರ್ ಖಂಡ್ರೆ), RDPR Minister", as printed; please verify. File no. RDC-EGS/591(3)/2026.
- The question says the ZP CEO reported 83 works satisfactory, and asks why vendors remain unpaid.
- The answer lists "serious deficiencies" (ಗಂಭೀರ ನ್ಯೂನತೆಗಳು) found by the Commissionerate inquiry team:
  - no photographs and no geotagged photographs in files;
  - no CIB (citizen information board) at sites;
  - measurement-book numbers in files differ from those in NMRs, so books were found to be **fake measurement books** (ನಕಲಿ ಅಳತೆ ಪುಸ್ತಕ);
  - site measurements do not tally with recorded ones;
  - some works were also paid for by the Gram Panchayat while PRED raised bills for the same works;
  - only 70 files were produced at inspection;
  - some files are "entirely fake" and some signatures look forged.
- The Project Director, ZP Koppal, further found:
  - full material FTOs generated when only about 50% of wages had been paid (the rule is 90%);
  - no three-stage geotag photos and no Bhuvan geotag photos;
  - no social audit, because files were not given to social audit for 5 years;
  - in **more than about 55 files**, suppliers registered for GST years after the works, yet quotations and bills are dated 2019;
  - quotations called within 5 days, and the L-1 bidder's name altered in comparative statements.
- The answer quotes CAG on KTPP Act tender violations.
- **Conclusion as printed:** payment "is not possible" (ಹಣ ಪಾವತಿಸಲು ಸಾಧ್ಯವಾಗಿರುವುದಿಲ್ಲ).
- No amount is stated in this answer.
- **Confidence:** High.

**(iii) Assembly 13082025/542, 13-08-2025.** Same MLA.
- Government accepts ("ಹೌದು") that the NREGA Assistant Director and Technical Coordinator in Kushtagi were not functioning properly.
- The Taluk Panchayat EO issued show-cause notices on 31.07.2025.
- **Confidence:** High.
