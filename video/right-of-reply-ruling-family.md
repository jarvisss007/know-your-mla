# Right of reply: The Ruling Family, Part 1 (draft for the channel owner to send)

**To:** Office of the Chief Minister of Karnataka (use the official contact on cm.karnataka.gov.in), with a copy to the Chief Minister's verified X account
**Subject:** Request for comment before publication: declared assets of Shri D.K. Shivakumar and Shri D.K. Suresh

Dear Sir/Madam,

I run Gandaberunda, an independent citizen channel that publishes short videos built only from public records. Before we publish a video about the declared assets of the Hon'ble Chief Minister and his brother, we would like to give the Chief Minister an opportunity to respond. We will include any reply we receive by **[date: 7 days from sending]**, in full, in the video description and on our website.

The video states:

1. In his sworn election affidavits, Shri D.K. Shivakumar declared total assets (with family) of Rs 75.60 crore in 2008, Rs 251.51 crore in 2013, Rs 840.02 crore in 2018 and Rs 1,413.80 crore in 2023 (ADR / myneta.info).
2. The Association for Democratic Reforms' July 2026 analysis of 31 sitting Chief Ministers names him the richest, at about Rs 1,413 crore.
3. In his Lok Sabha affidavits, Shri D.K. Suresh declared Rs 47.30 crore (2013), Rs 85.87 crore (2014), Rs 338.89 crore (2019) and Rs 593.05 crore (2024).
4. The CBI registered a case in October 2020 alleging assets of Rs 74.93 crore beyond known income for 2013-2018. In November 2023 the State cabinet withdrew consent for the CBI investigation and referred the case to the Lokayukta. The Supreme Court declined to quash the FIR in July 2024. No court has delivered a verdict.
5. The video also says that declared wealth includes revaluation of land and business holdings, that it is not proof of wrongdoing, and that neither brother has been convicted in any case.

Our questions:

1. What were the main sources of the growth in declared assets between 2008 and 2023?
2. What is the current status of the Lokayukta's investigation of the disproportionate-assets case, and has the Chief Minister been asked to appear or submit documents?
3. Does the Chief Minister wish to correct any figure above?

If any figure is wrong, please tell us and we will correct it before publishing.
