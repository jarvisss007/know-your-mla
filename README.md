# Gandaberunda: Know Your MLA

Gandaberunda is an independent, citizen-run publication that holds Karnataka's elected representatives to the public record. It is not affiliated with the Government of Karnataka or any political party, and does not use the official state emblem. Know Your MLA is its first project.


A searchable page showing what each of Karnataka's 224 MLAs declared in their 2023 election affidavit: wealth, debts, criminal cases, and change in declared wealth since 2018. Available in English and Kannada.

## What this is

- Every figure is self-declared by the MLA in a sworn affidavit to the Election Commission of India, as archived by the Association for Democratic Reforms at [myneta.info](https://www.myneta.info/Karnataka2023/).
- A declared case is not a conviction. Declared wealth is not evidence of wrongdoing.
- Entries in "Latest" and "On the record" link to the published report they come from. Allegations are attributed to the agency that made them.
- The page covers every party the same way.

## Known limits

- Figures are from the 2023 election; some seats have changed since.
- Asset figures were not available for 14 MLAs.
- Growth since 2018 is shown only for 81 re-elected MLAs who could be matched across both elections.
- Affidavit figures come from ADR's transcription, not the signed originals.

## Files

- `index.html` - the page (self-contained; data is embedded)
- `data/mlas.csv` - the same data as a spreadsheet
- `cards/` - shareable image cards
- `brand/` - the Gandaberunda mark, profile picture and banner (original artwork)

## Corrections

If a figure is wrong, open an issue with the MLA's name and a link to the affidavit. Errors will be fixed.
