## 1. Report No. 8 of 2025: Disaster Management

Revenue Department (Disaster Management) and KSNDMC. Period 2017-18 to 2022-23. Sampled districts: Belagavi, Chikkaballapura, Dakshina Kannada, Davanagere, Haveri, Kalaburagi, Kodagu, Ramanagara, Shivamogga. Government replies August 2024.

1. **Rs 421.60 crore** of State Disaster Mitigation Fund not used for mitigation. Rs 184.50 crore went to districts in 2020-21 with an instruction not to use it; Rs 210.80 crore for 2021-22 was retained by the State. Para 3.1.2, p.30. "non-execution of mitigative activities in the State". No reply printed.
2. **Rs 262.50 crore** unspent in Deputy Commissioners' PD accounts, plus **Rs 40.53 crore** with taluks, March 2023. Belagavi Rs 51.08 crore + Rs 22.18 crore; Kalaburagi Rs 44.35 crore + Rs 2.60 crore; Kodagu Rs 57.04 crore; Haveri Rs 27.58 crore. Para 3.1.4.1, Table 3.3, p.35. Expenditure "was overstated". Reply: directions issued to reconcile records.
3. **Rs 213.94 crore** first-instalment house-damage compensation (22,496 houses x Rs 95,100) where construction had not started, 2019-20 to 2022-23. Para 5.5.2, Table 5.5, p.74. "remained unfruitful". Reply: norms revised for 2024-25.
4. **Rs 138.72 crore** spent on the National Cyclone Risk Mitigation Project, coastal districts, 2015-16 to 2023-24. Para 7.1.1, pp.99-102.
   - Early-warning towers cut from 73 to 30; system untested; contractor M/s Broadcast Engineering Consultants India Limited.
   - Shelter equipment worth Rs 2.20 crore not supplied.
   - "rendered the entire expenditure unfruitful". Reply: reduced scope was approved centrally.
5. **Rs 135.21 crore** donations received and Rs 35.63 crore spent per bank pass sheets, against the department's figures of Rs 88.82 crore and Rs 1.52 crore (Aug 2019 to Sep 2023). Rs 2.34 crore of spending untraceable; no cashbook. Para 3.1.9, pp.39-40. "risk of embezzlement of funds going unnoticed". Reply: an audit team to probe.
6. **Rs 64.00 crore** compensation for 2,503 houses in eight taluks of Haveri in 2020-21, when rainfall was near or below normal. Para 5.5.2, p.73. 696 houses were upgraded from category C to B1/B2; CAG "could not rule out the possibility of fraudulent practices".
7. **Rs 6,752.28 crore** crop-loss input subsidy paid through the Parihara portal (5,91,302 cases), 2018-19 to 2022-23. This is the total paid; no irregular total is printed. Paras 5.5.3 and 5.5.3.1, p.76.
   - Random checks found double payments and payments above eligibility.
   - Haveri: FIRs (Feb 2020) against Tahsildars of all eight taluks over "fictitious bank accounts", no action in three years.
   - Reply: DC Haveri told to expedite.
8. **Rs 5,791.47 crore** drought expenditure 2017-18 to 2019-20 with no district-wise details, utilisation certificates or audit. Unnumbered paragraph under 3.1.8, p.39. "inadequate and thus, ineffective". Reply: relief paid through treasury from Kharif 2023.
9. **Rs 27.04 crore** idle in KSNDMC bank accounts (March 2023). **Rs 305.66 lakh** of KSNDMC spending "inadmissible under SDRF" (smart-city gauges Rs 281.53 lakh, chartered flight Rs 11.22 lakh, salary Rs 12.91 lakh). Para 3.1.4.2, Table 3.4, p.36. Reply: details awaited.
10. **Rs 7.69 crore** unutilised in the Fire and Emergency Department's bank account and **Rs 12 crore** lapsed in 2022-23; response units 67 to 96 per cent vacant. Paras 2.2.2 (pp.22-23) and 3.1.4.2 (p.37). "parking of SDRF grants outside the Government account".
11. **Belagavi district**: releases per district books exceed receipts per taluk books, 2017-18 to 2021-22. Raibag Rs 18.79 crore, Athani Rs 18.14 crore, Belagavi Rs 10.58 crore, Ramdurg Rs 1.18 crore, Hukkeri Rs 1.07 crore, Gokak Rs 0.55 crore, Kittur Rs 0.39 crore, Chikkodi Rs 0.14 crore; Rs 50.84 crore in all (my sum). Appendix 3.4, p.115; para 3.1.4.1, p.34. "fraught with the risk of misappropriation/embezzlement".
12. **Rs 1.37 crore** released to Sagara taluk (Shivamogga) not traceable; **Rs 1.03 crore** in Chikkaballapura taluk "kept in suspense for the past three years". Para 3.1.4.1, p.34. Reply: State Accounts staff to be engaged.
13. **Rs 18.59 lakh** misappropriated at the Tahsildar's office, Chintamani taluk, in 2017-18 through forged signatures on 59 cheques. Para 3.1.6, p.38. "Misappropriation of SDRF grants".
14. **Rs 1.81 crore** (Haveri taluks) and Rs 2.00 lakh (Jewargi, Kalaburagi) paid from SDRF for suicide and snake-bite deaths; Rs 25.00 lakh in Kalaburagi for an army death in Chhattisgarh. Para 3.1.7, pp.38-39. "ineligible cases". Reply: DCs to investigate.
15. **Works above the Rs 2 lakh SDRF limit**, "inadmissible under SDRF". Para 3.1.8, p.39; Appendix 3.5, pp.116-117.
    - Kalaburagi: Chittapur bridge repair Rs 53.81 lakh; city water-supply electricals Rs 28.13 lakh; Sedam road Rs 18.89 lakh; Sedam check dam Rs 17.99 lakh; Aland bridge Rs 9.76 lakh.
    - Belagavi: Savadatti school repairs, Rs 6 to 12 lakh each.
16. **Rs 107.84 crore** for preparedness and capacity building out of Rs 18,699.88 crore released in 2017-23. Para 3.1.4, Table 3.2, p.31. "very meagre (< one per cent)".
17. **Rs 2.45 crore** paid for a Krishna-basin flood early-warning system (M/s MOSERP Technologies India Private Limited); 6 of 15 sensors dead, no flood model. KSNDMC, 2021-23. Para 5.3.3.1, pp.61-62. "rendered largely unfruitful". Reply: penalty to be levied.
18. **Rs 1.09 crore** for wireless flood alerts to 104 gram panchayats in **Belagavi, Bagalkot and Raichur** (M/s Protronics Technologies; M/s Sumukha Technologies and Software Pvt Ltd); messages did not reach many GPs. Para 5.3.3.2, pp.62-63. "Unfruitful expenditure of Rs 1.09 crore". Reply: SIM cards being replaced.
19. **Rs 1.61 crore** on a Bengaluru urban flood model that does not exist ("Wasteful expenditure", para 5.3.3.3, p.63). **Rs 1.45 crore** of smart-city gauges and sensors in Mangaluru, **Belagavi and Hubballi-Dharwad**, 69 of 184 dead (para 5.3.3.4(b), p.64).
20. **Weather network**: 2,770 of 6,358 rain gauges (43 per cent) and 463 of 834 weather stations non-functional in December 2023; 153 gauges "stolen" with no police complaint. Para 4.3.2.1, pp.45-46. "negligence". Reply: maintenance contracts had lapsed.
21. **Rs 17.14 crore** approved (2016) to shift Mustur village, Ranebennur taluk, Haveri; Rs 3.30 crore released; 116 houses part-built. Para 5.6.1, pp.77-78. Belagavi: 209 villages, 10,03,262 people at recurrent flood risk and not relocated (para 5.4.1, p.67).
22. **Rs 6.25 crore** on a retaining wall at the DC office complex, Madikeri, which bulged and was dismantled. Para 6.5.3, pp.93-95. "Improper design resulted in failure of the retaining wall". Reply awaited.
23. **Rs 73.75 lakh** on a rescinded lightning-data contract (M/s Cost Prize Online India Pvt Ltd): "rendered the entire expenditure wasteful". Kalaburagi reported 61 lightning deaths in 2017-22 against 7 in the State plan. Paras 7.2.1.1-7.2.1.2, pp.104-105.

## 2. Report No. 7 of 2025: PMKKKY / District Mineral Foundation Trusts

Mines and Geology Department. Period 2015-16 to 2022-23. Sampled districts: Ballari, Chitradurga, Vijayanagara, Bengaluru Urban, Gadag. Replies August 2024. Koppal, Bagalkot, Belagavi, Raichur, Yadgir, Kalaburagi and Dharwad were not sampled.

25. **Rs 433.20 crore** of works approved in 2017-20 neither commenced nor tendered (403 works). Ballari: 27 works Rs 33.35 crore not commenced, 158 works Rs 325.15 crore not tendered. Para 5.11, Table 5.7, pp.48-49. "administrative lapses". Reply: Covid, technical reasons, local opposition.
26. **Rs 300.44 crore** allocated (Rs 133.77 crore spent) on 212 works "not envisaged in the guidelines". Ballari 169 works, Rs 255.37 crore; Vijayanagara 31 works, Rs 34.47 crore; Chitradurga 9 works, Rs 10.25 crore. Para 5.2, pp.27-31. Table heading "inadmissible works executed".
27. Within that, **Rs 152.06 crore** on 96 "physical infrastructure" works (auditorium, high-mast lamps, police station, new DC office, stadiums) and **Rs 119.78 crore** on 107 "education" works (BEO office, buses for KKRTC, taluk stadiums). Para 5.2, pp.28-29. Reply: DMF approval was obtained; "not acceptable".
28. Also within that: **Rs 19.00 crore** on three electric sub-stations in Sandur taluk, Ballari (p.30), and **Rs 3.20 crore** on zoo enclosures (pp.27-28). CAG rejects the zoo reply.
29. **Rs 78.15 crore to Rs 234.47 crore** DMF contribution on royalty routed through works departments that nobody could confirm was deducted (Ballari base Rs 32.45 crore, Chitradurga Rs 73.55 crore, Vijayanagara Rs 15.92 crore). Para 3.5, pp.16-17. "no system of obtaining returns". Reply: mechanism to be developed.
30. **Rs 81.66 crore** royalty and **Rs 14.16 crore** DMF contribution not demanded on minerals moved without permits, though Rs 516.60 crore penalty was levied, 2018-19 to 2021-22. Chitradurga penalty Rs 313.11 crore; Vijayanagara Rs 137.50 crore; Ballari Rs 19.67 crore. Para 3.6, Table 3.3, pp.17-18. Reply: rules give no DMF on penalty cases; "not acceptable".
31. **Rs 98.86 crore** left in treasury PD accounts, July 2018 to March 2023 (Ballari Rs 37.36 crore, Chitradurga Rs 59.41 crore, Gadag Rs 2.09 crore). Para 4.1, p.19. "probable loss of interest of Rs 14.09 crore". Reply: transfer to be initiated.
32. **Rs 252.62 crore** Ballari endowment fund not placed in fixed deposits from June 2021; interest of **Rs 7.58 crore** "could have earned". Para 4.4, p.21. Reply silent.
33. **Rs 20.00 crore** allocated by DMFT Ballari as beneficiary share under PMAY (Urban); **Rs 7.26 crore** released (Sept 2022) to Rajiv Gandhi Rural Housing Corporation without Government approval. Para 5.3, p.31. "Irregular allocation of DMF Funds". Reply: post-facto approval to be sought; "not acceptable".
34. **Priority skew**: Rs 1,085.07 crore of Rs 1,329 crore "other priority" money (81.65 per cent) went to physical infrastructure; environment got 3.88 per cent of high-priority allocation. Para 5.1, pp.25-27. "lacks justification".

37. **Rs 4.00 crore** of computers for Holalkere taluk schools (KEONICS): printers dead from installation in all 38 schools inspected; a board credited the work to "MLA grant". Para 5.7.2, p.40.
38. **Rs 2.09 crore** on a 19-village drinking-water project near Manangi, Chitradurga, with no permission to tap the source. Para 5.6.1, pp.37-38. "entire expenditure of Rs 2.09 crore remained unfruitful".
39. **Rs 1.50 crore** on a sheep-breeding centre at Kurekuppa, Sandur taluk, Ballari (given to KRIDL without tender), incomplete after seven years. Para 5.9.1, pp.44-45. "unfruitful expenditure of Rs 1.50 crore". Reply: completion targeted September 2024.

## 6. Report No. 13 of 2025: MGNREGS (full body read by me)
