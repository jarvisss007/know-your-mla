## Before filing (owner)

- **Address:** confirm the PIO before posting. Since the 2025 restructuring, Package-3 and Package-04 (West Zone white-topping) and the Malleshwaram division may sit with the West city corporation rather than the Greater Bengaluru Authority. Section 6(3) obliges a wrong office to forward it, but the right address saves a month. Two separate applications, one per office, are also fine; split the table by work code.
- **Fee:** Rs 10 per application. Keep the postal or court-fee receipt.
- **Send** from a personal address, never a company mailbox. Keep a scan of the signed application and the receipt in the private repo (`MLA-ARE-BADDDDD`, cases/).
- **Dates:** the reply is due 30 days after the office receives it. If there is no reply or a refusal, the first appeal goes to the First Appellate Authority (the officer senior to the PIO) within 30 days of that.
- **Log** the filing date in `ops/log/`.
