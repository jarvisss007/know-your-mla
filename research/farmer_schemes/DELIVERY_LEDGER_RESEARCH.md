## How to read this file

- Every row: item | on paper | on the ground | source URL | source date | quote (<15 words, verbatim as read) | confidence | which government.
- Confidence: HIGH = I read the figure in the primary document or a fetched article body. MEDIUM = read in one fetched secondary article, or a primary figure whose date/definition is ambiguous. LOW = search-snippet only, headline only, or figures that conflict across sources. A "blank quote" means I did not read the sentence verbatim, so I give none.
- Government key. Centre: BJP-led NDA (Modi) throughout. Party labels are background knowledge, not taken from the sources, except where a source says so.
- "Figure" mismatches between outlets are listed in the row, not smoothed over.

## 10. Eight strongest rows for a public page

All eight have a hard figure on both sides, an identifiable source and a stated date, and each carries a fairness caveat.

1. **Drought 2023-24, ask vs release.** Karnataka asked the NDRF for Rs 18,171.44 crore (memorandum timeline Sep to Nov 2023). The Centre approved about Rs 3,454 crore (Apr 2024), after the state went to the Supreme Court. HIGH. Centre BJP; Karnataka Congress. Caveat: Rs 12,577 crore of the ask was "gratuitous relief" outside the usual input-subsidy head; reports give Rs 3,454 / 3,498.8 / "around 3,400" crore.
2. **MSP payments, KMS 2025-26.** Procured value Rs 4,422.17 crore; Centre released Rs 190.10 crore (9 Jul 2026), with Rs 1,902.58 crore older reimbursement pending back to KMS 2009-10. HIGH on figures as reported, single outlet. Centre BJP; Karnataka Congress; legacy dues span governments.
3. **Milk incentive arrears.** Rs 800 crore pending since Sep 2023 (30 May 2024), Rs 606.69 crore for Jun to Oct 2024 (18 Dec 2024); the minister's year-wise list shows dues of Rs 54 cr (2019) to Rs 208 cr (2023). HIGH. Both BJP and Congress governments.
4. **Micro-irrigation, CAG.** Target 15.70 lakh ha vs 11.81 lakh ha achieved (75 percent) in 2017-21; Rs 3,979.39 crore released and Rs 3,895.45 crore spent; state had not released its 57 percent share in full. HIGH. Spans three governments; hit 103 percent in the final year.
5. **Agriculture grant, CAG.** 2020-23: 5.3 to 9.7 percent of the revenue provision unspent each year; 2023-24 spending Rs 6,653.64 crore against an original budget of Rs 5,748.81 crore (15.74 percent over). HIGH. A good "budget is not the same as delivery, in both directions" row.
6. **2025 flood relief.** Loss about Rs 10,748 crore over 14.58 lakh ha; state transferred Rs 2,251.63 crore to 14.24 lakh farmers; asked Centre for Rs 614.90 crore; 44,208 farmers still unpaid in Dec 2025. HIGH for the first three, MEDIUM-LOW for the 44,208. Karnataka Congress; Centre BJP.
7. **PM-KISAN, eligible farmers dropped.** More than 30,000 (31,340 per DH headline) eligible farmers unpaid Aug to Nov 2025; Centre said 22,000 added to the 22nd instalment (14 Feb 2026). HIGH for the 30,000 and 22,000 (Hans India); MEDIUM for 31,340. Centre BJP; Karnataka Congress.
8. **2026 drought, relief promised vs credited.** Karnataka asked the Centre for Rs 22,400.58 crore (3 Nov 2026) and began paying a state Rs 2,500 first instalment (5 Nov); farmers in Yadgir and Gadag reported Rs 1,500 or Rs 2,000 (10 Nov). MEDIUM-HIGH. Fresh and unresolved, so frame it as "so far".

Close seconds if a row needs replacing: PM-KISAN 2022 ineligible payments (Rs 442 crore paid, Rs 7.71 crore recovered; MEDIUM; BJP both levels); zero-interest loan actual vs target (Rs 15,960 crore / 21 lakh vs Rs 27,000 crore / 37 lakh; MEDIUM).
