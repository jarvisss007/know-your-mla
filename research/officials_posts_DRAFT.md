**Legislative Assembly, unstarred question 1324, Munirathna, answered 10 Mar 2026 by the Chief Minister** (https://kla.kar.nic.in/assembly/answers/16assembly/pdf/10032026/1324.pdf). Period 10 Sep 2022 to 28 Feb 2026, IAS, IPS, KAS and other senior officers: 104 raid cases + 91 trap cases = 195; 89 under investigation; no conviction and no acquittal. The "62 stayed by the High Court" figure reported by The News Minute is not in the cover answer; it can only come from counting the 25-page annexure, which we have not done. Cover answer read by a second pass only.

**Corrections to the press reports.** Organiser (12 Sep 2026) and Vartha Bharati (4 Sep 2026) merged the two Council replies and got three things wrong: they called the 939 trap cases "disproportionate-assets cases" (assets cases are 309); they presented the class table as assets cases when it is a headcount across all 1,248 cases; and they gave Group A as 12 when the reply says 212. They also left out the 24 public representatives. Do not quote either outlet for these figures; quote the reply.

**Sanction to prosecute** (not yet checked against a primary document): 84 officials in 22 cases awaiting permission (Organiser, 4 Jun 2026, citing unnamed Lokayukta documents; weak); 206 officials across 27 departments awaiting sanction in 2024 (PTI, 5 Oct 2024, citing a Lokayukta official).

**How long a case can take.** In March 2026 a special court for Lokayukta cases convicted a former managing director of the Karnataka Council for Technical Upgradation in an assets case with a check period of December 1982 to July 2014 (three years' rigorous imprisonment, Rs 4.5 crore fine), and asked the investigating officer and two official witnesses to explain lapses, criticising years of delay (Deccan Herald, 31 Mar 2026, https://www.deccanherald.com/india/karnataka/da-case-special-court-seeks-explanation-from-three-officials-for-misleading-report-3951322).

Not done: the annexures (district-wise and department-wise tables, 200+ pages each) have not been read.

**The story in one line:** many raids, very few finished trials. The choke points are (a) government sanction to prosecute and (b) court stays.

## 3. Case file: Excise Department (the best-documented chain in 2026)

- 17 Jan 2026: Lokayukta police arrested Excise Deputy Commissioner (Bengaluru Urban) Jagadeesh Naik, Superintendent K M Thammanna and constable Lakkappa Gani while allegedly accepting Rs 25 lakh. Complainant said Rs 80 lakh was first demanded for a CL-7 bar licence, and more for a microbrewery licence. Naik and Thammanna suspended 21 Jan 2026. (The News Minute, 5 Feb 2026; Deccan Herald)
- The complainant alleged the money was meant to reach "the minister and other officials". Opposition leader R. Ashoka alleged fixed rates per licence type and a Rs 5,000-6,000 crore scam; the Wine Merchants' Association made similar allegations. **Excise Minister R.B. Timmapur denied everything as false and political and refused to resign.** No agency finding names him in anything read so far.
- June 2026: ED searched 14 premises in a case concerning Additional Commissioner (Belagavi Division) Y D Manjunath. Reported seizures: Rs 5.5 crore cash, Rs 7.8 crore gold, foreign currency, and over 100 excise licences in other people's names. ED described middlemen collecting monthly payments from liquor outlets. (Free Press Journal, 26 Jun 2026; ED press release dated 25 Jun 2026, not yet read directly.)

## 4. Paying for a posting ("transfer business")

- Almost all of this is allegation by opposition politicians (H.D. Kumaraswamy's "transfer syndicate" claim, 2023; BJP claims in the PSI Parashuram death case, 2024). No conviction found.
- One documented action: 31 Aug 2026, three sub-inspectors in Uttara Kannada suspended after an audio clip about money allegedly paid for a posting at Haliyal station; a preliminary inquiry found prima facie misconduct. (The South First, 1 Sep 2026)
- Recruitment: PSI recruitment case (545 posts; CID chargesheet against a then-ADGP) and the 2026 KPSC veterinary officer recruitment case (an IAS officer arrested; CID said candidates paid Rs 40-80 lakh; High Court reportedly moved the probe to an SIT). Status of the PSI case still needs checking.
- KPSC veterinary officer recruitment (exam of 9 Jan 2026, 400 posts): on 16 Nov 2026 the ED filed a prosecution complaint under the PMLA before a special court in Bengaluru against four: the former KPSC chairman, a former member, the former controller of examinations (an IAS officer) and an alleged middleman. ED's alleged proceeds of crime: Rs 4.17 cr, Rs 1.48 cr, Rs 1.52 cr and Rs 1.52 cr (total Rs 8.69 cr). Three are in judicial custody; Rs 1.22 cr of property was provisionally attached on 13 Nov 2026. These are the agency's allegations, not yet tested in court. (ETV Bharat, 16 Nov 2026, https://www.etvbharat.com/en/state/kpsc-recruitment-scam-ed-files-prosecution-complaint-against-four-uncovers-alleged-money-laundering-worth-rs-8-dot-69-crore-enn26111604894; The Hindu, 14 Nov 2026, on the attachment.) Added to the tracker on 16 Nov 2026 (rows 2026-08-29-1, 2026-10-06-1, 2026-11-16-1; the alleged middleman, a private person, is left out). This is the first 2026 case found where an agency has filed a charge in court.

## 6. What a citizen publication can actually do (accountability, not accusation)

1. **Track outcomes, not raids.** Build a public table: raid date, post, department, claimed value, then chargesheet / sanction / stay / verdict. The gap between column 1 and the last column is the story, and it is all from records.
2. **Sanction watch.** RTI to DPAR and each department: list of Lokayukta sanction requests pending, with dates received. Delay is a decision by a named authority.
3. **Property returns.** Officials must file annual property statements (Conduct Rules; Lokayukta Act s.22 for other public servants). The government has given the Assembly lists of IAS/KAS officers who did not file. Ask for the current list by RTI or through an MLA's question.
4. **Service-counter evidence.** Sub-registrar, RTO, taluk office, BESCOM: collect readers' written, dated experiences and forward them to the Lokayukta as complaints (Form I and II with affidavit). The owner files; we do not publish names from tips.
5. **Legislature questions.** Ask friendly MLAs/MLCs to put unstarred questions: department-wise cases, sanctions pending, convictions.
