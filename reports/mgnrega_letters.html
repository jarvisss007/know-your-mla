Ask for the records

Anyone in India can ask the government for its records for a fee of Rs 10. Below are four ready letters you can fill in and send about your own village, then the full requests behind One tank, many works. They ask for records, not conclusions.

How to file
  1. Copy a letter, fill in the parts in [square brackets], print and sign it.
  2. Attach a Rs 10 Indian Postal Order (from any post office) made out as the letter says, or pay as your state's rules allow. Families below the poverty line pay nothing if they attach proof.
  3. Send it by registered or speed post to the Public Information Officer named, and keep the receipt.
  4. A reply is due in 30 days. If none comes, or it is refused, you can file a first appeal with the senior officer of the same office within 30 days, and after that with the Karnataka Information Commission.

To find a work code, look up your block and panchayat on the Bhuvan-BHUGRAM dashboard (report "Accepted Geotags"), or use the lists on our pages. Asking for records is your right; you do not have to give a reason.
