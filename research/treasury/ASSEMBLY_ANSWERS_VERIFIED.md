# Independent second read: Karnataka Legislative Assembly answers (16th Assembly)

Method: all 15 PDFs downloaded from kla.kar.nic.in with curl, every page rendered to an image and read by eye, with zoomed crops for tables and rotated annexures. All are scans with no usable text layer. Where a table carries totals, I re-added my own row readings and compared them to the printed total; the result is stated per answer. Nothing was written to the project; page images are in the session scratchpad at `C:\Users\binap\AppData\Local\Temp\claude\968a6c9bf8b21e83\38fe97c8-650c-4cf3-b6bc-9c44aef7b055\scratchpad\img` (temporary).

Common labels: *bāki* = pending; *pāvati* = payment; *bidugade* = release; *vecca* = expenditure; *hanchike* = allocation; *oṭṭu* = total; *rū. kōṭigaḷalli* = Rs crore; *rū. lakṣagaḷalli* = Rs lakh.

## Things to know before using these figures

1. **16122025/1542 is not "minor irrigation bills" in general.** It covers only one account head ("4711 taḍegōḍe niyantraṇa"). Pending there is Rs 35,389.21 **lakh** (about Rs 353.89 crore) at end November 2025. It cannot be set beside the Rs 3,762 crore Minor Irrigation figure in Q.401.
2. **17082026/167 contains no statewide RDPR dues figure and no rupee total of dues.** It concerns unpaid MGNREGA material suppliers for check-dam works in Kushtagi taluk (Koppal) in 2019-20, and concludes payment is not possible.
3. **Capital expenditure differs between two Chief Minister answers.** Q.422 (09.12.2025) gives 57,953.20 / 52,378.86 / 60,601.15 / 81,554.38 for 2022-23 to 2025-26. Q.1658 (17.03.2026) gives 61,204 / 56,852 / 68,233 / 83,200 for the same years. Q.907 gives 56,851 for 2023-24. Neither document explains the basis.
4. **PWD pending total is printed three ways:** 8,804.45 crore in the answer text (1355, 1394, 1495); 8,80,440.99 lakh (8,804.41 crore) in the head-wise annexure; 8,80,445.00 lakh in the division-wise annexure, whose rows add to 8,80,445.14.
5. **09122025/6:** the "paid" figure is printed as "rū.11583.34 gaḷāgiddu" with no "crore" after it; the pending figure does carry "kōṭi". The annexures are not in this PDF (only a "Click here for Annexures" link stamp).
6. **15122025/1355:** the paid period is printed as 01-06-2023 to "31-11-2025", a date that does not exist.
7. **SCSP/TSP guarantee figures differ between 1777 (Finance, Dec 2025) and 2550 (Social Welfare, Mar 2026)** for the same years, e.g. 2024-25 Gruha Lakshmi SCSP 5,736 vs 5,879.85. Details under 2550.
