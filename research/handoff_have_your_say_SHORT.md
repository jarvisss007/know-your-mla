## Things the Short must NOT say

- Don't say the satellite pages show forest loss, land grabs or construction. On all three seats the date check shows most change is season and rainfall. The bare-ground patches are "new bare or built-up ground (proxy)": cause unknown.
- Don't name anyone as responsible for any patch. Don't imply a party link to any patch because of who holds the seat.
- Don't promise a reply to every message or a deadline for any satellite request.
- Field visits: say "never enter private land, never photograph people".
