## WELFARE SECTION

### W1. Ration grain not reaching households: Karnataka 46.4% (2011-12) to 6.2% (2022-23)
- Claim on page: 2011-12 46.4%; 2022-23 6.2%; note "India as a whole lost about 28% in 2022-23. A second research estimate puts Karnataka at 17% that year; the methods differ."
- Verdict: **CONFIRMED + CAVEAT (source fix and comparability caveat needed)**
- Where each number lives (they are in two different papers):
  - 6.2% (2022-23): ICRIER policy brief "Rationalising Public Distribution System in India" (Das, Roy, Gulati), Nov 2024, Annexure 1 and Figure 4. URL: https://icrier.org/pdf/pb-27.pdf . Row: "Karnataka 2.60 2.44 0.16 6.2" (MMT offtake, consumption, leakage gap, %). All-India "almost 28 percent" confirmed in the abstract.
  - 46.4% (2011-12): ICRIER Working Paper 294, Gulati and Saini, 2015, Table 1. URL: https://icrier.org/pdf/Working_Paper_294.pdf . Row: "Karnataka 1.62 3.01 1.40 46.4%".
  - The page's source link points only at the 2024 brief. **The 46.4% is not in that brief.** The brief names WP 294 in its references only.
- Supporting quote (2024 brief, footnote 6): "not strictly comparable due to differences in the weighting used" (under 15 words; the footnote is about exactly this two-paper comparison).
- Notes:
  - The 2024 brief says the two analyses differ in weights, in whether other welfare schemes are included, and in whether state offtake is added. So the 46.4 to 6.2 fall is a cross-paper comparison, not one consistent series. The page already warns about methods for the 17% figure but not for this pair.
  - The "17%" is real: Pingali and Puri, Ideas for India, 11 Aug 2025, Table 2: Karnataka 34.7 (NSS 2011-12), 17 (HCES 2022-23), 1.9 (HCES 2023-24). URL: https://www.ideasforindia.in/topics/poverty-inequality/declining-pds-leakages-a-look-at-the-numbers.html . Note that on that series the 2011-12 starting point is 34.7, not 46.4. The page does not give a link for the 17%.
  - Minor: Figure 4 in the brief shows West Bengal 9.0 and Annexure 1 shows 8.7 (internal inconsistency, not about Karnataka, ignore).

- Claim on page: "Required for 105 diversions, 2013-18: 911 ha"; "Raised: 450 ha"; 49.3%.
- Verdict: **CONFIRMED** (exact figures are 911.13 ha and 449.57 ha)
- Source: CAG Report No. 3 of 2019 (Economic Sector, Karnataka), chapter 3, para 3.2.2.1 and Table 3.1, pp. 99-101. Tabled 10 Oct 2019 (per cag.gov.in report page). Chapter PDF: https://cag.gov.in/uploads/download_audit_report/2019/Chapter_3_Compliance_Audit_of_Report_No_3_of_2019_Economic_Sector_Government_of_Karnataka.pdf . Landing page: https://cag.gov.in/en/audit-report/details/55704
- Quote: "105 cases ... 817.83 ha of forest land was diverted and 911.13 ha was stipulated for CA" and "only 449.57 ha (49 per cent) was raised."
- Notes:
  - Report wording: 911.13 ha "stipulated for CA" by MoEF. The page's "required" is fair; "stipulated" is the auditor's word. CAG calls the shortfall 461.56 ha (51 per cent).
  - Page cites "para 3.2"; the precise paragraph is 3.2.2.1 (section 3.2 is the thematic audit, with the CA table in 3.2.2.1).
  - In the 10 test-checked divisions the shortfall was 157.83 ha (33.56%). The page uses the statewide figure; correct.
  - The page's note "afforestation fund closed 2023-24 with Rs 764.85 crore unspent" is confirmed in substance: CAG State Finances 2023-24 (Report No. 4 of 2025), para 2.6.4: opening balance Rs 661.93 crore, Rs 391.42 crore credited, Rs 288.51 crore spent, "closing balance to Rs 764.85 crore."
