# NOTE (not a finding): how long bills wait, and the ones that did not

Status: DRAFT, 17 Nov 2026. From the corporation's payment records (OpenCity), earliest payment listing per bill, 14,256 bills.

## What the records show

- The median bill waited **561 days** from its bill-register date to payment. Medians by payment quarter run from 247 to 729 days.
- Of 2,546 bills of Rs 1 crore or more, the median wait was 494 days, but **114 were paid within 60 days (Rs 424.5 crore)**.
- Splitting those large bills by whether the description names an earmarked source or priority programme (white-topping, Tender SURE, GoK grants, Amrut/Nagarothana, Smart City, World Bank, K-100, garbage and transport hire): earmarked bills had a median wait of 328 days and 24 paid within 60 days (Rs 127.3 crore); **ordinary works had a median wait of 602 days and 90 paid within 60 days (Rs 297.2 crore)**.
- The lakes division (code 308) shows several large bills paid in 11 to 49 days while other works waited one to three years.

## Why this is not yet a finding

The rules on payment order changed and are disputed:
- A Finance Department order is reported to require bills to be cleared by a seniority list.
- A later Urban Development Department order is reported to have allowed BBMP to clear 75% of bills by general seniority, with 25% on a "random basis" (Deccan Herald, 2023, not opened).
- The High Court in late 2023 questioned the seniority system in a contempt case on unpaid bills and asked the government to issue guidelines (PTI via South First and Udayavani).

So a bill paid in weeks may sit lawfully in the non-seniority share, or be state-funded with its own release. The data cannot tell.

## What would settle it (RTI to the Chief Accounts Officer, owner files)

1. The seniority list of pending bills as maintained in 2024-25 and 2025-26, and the rule or order under which payments were made out of seniority.
2. For the 90 large ordinary bills paid within 60 days (list in `data/fast_paid_large_bills.csv`), the note or order approving each payment out of turn and the officer who approved it.
3. The selection record for any "random basis" share: how bills were picked, and by whom.
