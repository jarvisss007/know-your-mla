  Source: CitizenNest guide, updated 20 Jun 2026, https://www.citizennest.com/guide/ka-krishi-bhagya

- Same 80/90 split reported for non-polyhouse items in an undated Deccan Herald Mandya report (old, pre-2023 relaunch). LOW.
- Budget: "Krishi Bhagya Scheme, launched 2014 (revitalised 2023), Rs 440+ Cr" in IMPRI 2026-27 list, 4 Sep 2026,
  https://www.impriindia.com/?p=75134 . MEDIUM for existence/allocation only.
- Polythene lining separately, fencing: not found. Taluk/district list: not found; only "dry and drought-prone taluks, priority" (LOW, CitizenNest).
- Note: a "12000 farm ponds" budget line was cited by a search summary (source not opened); the draft's 32,138 is from the 2026-27 budget text. Do not mix.

## 8. KCC 4% subvention and Rs 5 lakh
- Partly resolved. The official PIB backgrounder "Kisan Credit Card: Fueling Growth in Agriculture", 11 March 2026 (static.pib.gov.in/WriteReadData/specificdocs/documents/2026/mar/doc2026311819801.pdf, read locally) says:
  "Under MISS, the loan limit has been enhanced to Rs 5 lakh, with collateral-free credit raised to Rs 2 lakh" and loans at 7% with "additional 3% subvention for timely repayment, reducing the effective rate to 4%". HIGH that Rs 5 lakh is stated as in force at PIB level.
- Conflict: RBI circular of 13 Jan 2026 (FY 2025-26) reportedly still shows overall limit Rs 3 lakh (sub-limit Rs 2 lakh) and 1.5% bank subvention (https://website.rbi.org.in/documents/d/rbi/circularonmissdatedjanuary132026 , not readable, seen via search summary; MEDIUM). Aug 2026 report (AngelOne) also says Rs 3 lakh and a proposed 50 bp cut. No FY 2026-27 RBI MISS circular found. Rs 5 lakh coverage for 4% effective rate: unconfirmed. Safe wording: "up to Rs 3 lakh sure; Rs 5 lakh announced, ask your bank."
