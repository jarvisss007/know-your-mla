Paper vs Ground

What Karnataka's records say was sanctioned, released or recovered, set beside what the same records say actually happened. Every row comes from an auditor, Parliament, a government statement or a named press report, and says which.

Amounts are in rupees crore (1 crore = 10 million). Lakh = 100,000. Percentages on the bars are our arithmetic on the two figures shown.

Money released, work done

Public works and central schemes. All figures here are from Comptroller and Auditor General (CAG) reports unless marked.

Welfare that leaked

The ration system and the guarantee schemes. Grain leakage has fallen sharply; the open problem is who is on the lists.

Forest and land

Forest diverted, land recovered from encroachers, and land the state acquired and left unused.

Held back from this draft

  • Recent MLA local-area fund (KLLADS) figures. Nothing newer than 2018 could be opened at its source; the state has no public portal for these works.
  • The rupee total in the 2025 Justice Nagamohan Das report on 761 Bengaluru works. One newspaper carries a figure; the wire report of the same event carries none.
  • The "40% commission" inquiry. Press reports say the commission found no clear proof of a fixed 40% but "some truth" to commission claims. We have not seen the report itself.
  • Reported 2026 audit observations on ineligible ration cards and Gruha Lakshmi bank accounts. They rest on one outlet's account of an audit that has not been tabled.
  • Any district-by-district table of forest encroachment. None was found in a document we could open.
