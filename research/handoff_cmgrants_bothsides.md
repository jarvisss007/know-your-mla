## EN
**The BJP government did the same.** In February 2022 Chief Minister Basavaraj Bommai told the Assembly that ₹3,322 crore of "special grant" had gone to 64 seats: 63 got exactly ₹52 crore each. On the date of his answer, 63 of those seats were held by the BJP and one by an independent who backed the government; not one was held by Congress or JD(S). Asked whether he would correct the disparity and give every constituency the grant, he answered: "Does not arise." Opposition seats such as Yemkanamardi and Varuna got ₹25 crore each, from May 2022. So the ₹50 and ₹25 crore tiers did not start in 2025: the party in power has funded its own seats at about double, under both governments.
Sources: Assembly unstarred question 114, answered 15 Feb 2022 (https://kla.kar.nic.in/assembly/answers/pdf/15022022/114.pdf); questions 297 (20 Dec 2022) and 1097 (21 Feb 2023).
