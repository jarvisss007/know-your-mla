1. **Same description under two or more work codes (construction only).** 38 cases. Most are recurring ward items (tractor labour, emergency reserve, pothole filling) that get a new code each year, which is normal. Two worth a line:
   - *Begur Road widening, Ch. 0-3,250 m, Phase-1*: identical description under 304-19-000013 (bill 583412, Rs 3.85 cr) and 304-20-000205 (bills 652823, Rs 3.13 cr; 729380, Rs 6.64 cr), same contractor. Could be a fresh order for balance work. On bill 729380, Rs 3.64 cr of Rs 6.64 cr (55%) was deducted, far above the usual 4-18%, so something was withheld or recovered. Ask, if a Begur Road RTI is ever filed.
   - *Ward 193 Package-2* (three layouts, three work codes, same contractor, all billed 6 Jul 2021 at Rs 94.0, 89.1 and 88.7 lakh): one tendered package with a code per layout, a common practice. Not splitting on this evidence.
2. **Deductions.** For 9,508 bills of Rs 10 lakh or more the median deduction is 7.7% (5th percentile 3.7%). Statutory deductions on a works bill (income-tax TDS 1-2%, GST TDS 2%, construction workers' cess 1%) should come to about 4% or more. 184 works bills (Rs 64.9 cr) show under 3%, many exactly 2%. The likely gap is about 2% of Rs 64.9 cr, roughly Rs 1.3 cr, and it could be deducted on a different bill or exempt. Low value.
