## The punchline facts (HIGH)
1. **1,248 → 0.** Lokayukta corruption cases against officials, 10 Sep 2022 to 13 Aug 2026: 1,248 (939 bribery traps, 309 assets cases). Government servants held guilty in them so far: none.
   - On screen: "CM's written reply, Legislative Council Q.452(628), 20 Aug 2026".
   - ಕನ್ನಡ: "1,248 ಪ್ರಕರಣಗಳು. ಇದುವರೆಗೆ ತಪ್ಪಿತಸ್ಥರೆಂದು ಸಾಬೀತಾದ ಸರ್ಕಾರಿ ನೌಕರರು: ಯಾರೂ ಇಲ್ಲ."
2. **195 → 0.** Cases against IAS, IPS, KAS and other senior officers, 10 Sep 2022 to 28 Feb 2026: 195 (104 raids, 91 traps). All pending; none ended in conviction or acquittal; 89 still under investigation.
   - On screen: "Assembly Q.1324, 10 Mar 2026 (signed by then CM Siddaramaiah)".
3. **The unanswered question.** On 25 Aug 2026, MLAs asked in the Assembly how many prosecutions are waiting for the government's permission, and for how long (Q.1558 and Q.1652, DPAR). As of 23 Nov 2026, neither answer is on the Assembly website, though the answers to neighbouring questions are.
   - Say "no answer published", not "the government refused".
4. **The law's clock.** A department should decide a request to prosecute within 3 months, plus 1 for legal advice (Prevention of Corruption Act s.19). The Supreme Court held the limit binding (Vijay Rajmohan, 11 Oct 2022).
