# DRAFT: Did the BJP government (2019-23) also tier development money by party? (24 Nov 2026)

Status: DRAFT, not for the site until re-checked. Companion to `research/follow_the_money/CM_GRANTS_BY_SEAT_DRAFT.md` and `PRAGATHI_PATHA_BY_SEAT_DRAFT.md`. Tables in this folder; every lookup in SOURCES.md.

## Verdict
**Same pattern, on the records found.** Assembly unstarred question 114 (answered 15 Feb 2022, signed by Chief Minister Basavaraj Bommai, Finance Department file FD 18 FC-2/2022) lists a Rs 3,322.12 crore special grant to 64 seats: all held by BJP MLAs, plus Mulbagal (Independent H. Nagesh, who backed the BJP government). No Congress or JD(S) seat is on it. 63 of the 64 got exactly Rs 52 crore (Rs 50 crore of works plus Rs 2 crore for temples); Gundlupet got Rs 46.12 crore. Asked whether the "discrimination" would be corrected and the grant given to all seats, the government answered "does not arise". Later answers show two Congress seats (Yemkanmardi, Varuna) given Rs 25 crore under the CM's special grant, with Finance orders of May 2022. That is a 50-versus-25 split by party, as under today's CMIDP, with the parties reversed. There is no consolidated official list of the 2022 opposition round.

Confidence: 64-seat table HIGH; Rs 25 crore for the two named Congress seats HIGH; "opposition seats got Rs 25 crore" as a general rule MEDIUM.

Athani and Kagwad: 2019 defectors (Congress MLAs disqualified and re-elected as BJP, Dec 2019). Sira (2020, was JD(S)), Basavakalyan (2021, was Congress), Sindgi (2021, was JD(S)) and Ranebennur (2019, was KPJP/Congress) were won by the BJP in by-elections. Sirsi is the Speaker's seat. - Two readings (110 dpi; 160 dpi on fresh crops) agree on every PWD, RDPR and Minor Irrigation row, the small tables on pp. 13-14, and Muzrai rows 1-8. Muzrai rows 9-64 read once: every cell 2.00, printed total 128.00 = 64 x 2.00. The flat Rs 52.00 per seat cross-checks each amount against its seat.

- Link to the CM's special grant: Q.2103 (7 Mar 2022, question text only) refers to "Rs 2 crore to temples of each constituency via Muzrai under the CM's special grant 2021-22", matching the Muzrai rows. Q.114's own answer page does not use the phrase.

## 2. Opposition seats, 2022
- Yemkanmardi (Congress, Satish Jarkiholi), HIGH: Q.297, 20 Dec 2022, signed Bommai: Rs 25.00 crore under the CM's special grant (RDPR 6.5, PWD 10, Social Welfare 1.5, ST Welfare 3, Kannada & Culture 1, Muzrai 1, Tourism 2); Finance concurrences 9-11 May 2022. https://kla.kar.nic.in/assembly/answers/pdf/20122022/297.pdf pp. 2-3.
- Varuna (Congress, Yathindra Siddaramaiah), HIGH: Q.1097, 21 Feb 2023: Rs 25.00 crore under the CM's special grant, Finance order 10 May 2022, Social Welfare share Rs 2.25 crore confirmed. https://kla.kar.nic.in/assembly/answers/pdf/21022023/1097.pdf p. 1.

Party labels in the CSV are from memory and must be checked against ECI before use; by them, opposition seats were part of the 2022-23 round. https://kla.kar.nic.in/assembly/answers/pdf/15022023/546.pdf pp. 3-5.

## 3. Press (MEDIUM unless marked)
- Deccan Herald, 16 Sep 2019: Yediyurappa "is understood to have" given Rs 427 crore to 79 BJP legislators; Congress and JD(S) allocations cut. Unnamed officials (MEDIUM-LOW).
- Deccan Herald, 3 Mar 2022: Bengaluru grants from Bommai's Rs 6,000 crore: Rs 2,718 crore first to 15 BJP seats only (about Rs 181 crore each); after protests Rs 943 crore to 13 mostly Congress seats (about Rs 72 crore each). Cites the Urban Development Department order of 25 Feb 2022.
- Deccan Herald, 11 Mar 2023: Rs 255 crore from the CM's discretionary share of the BBMP budget to 11 BJP seats only.
- Deccan Herald, 4 Aug 2021: Yediyurappa released Rs 1,277 crore to 132 seats on 19 Jul 2021, including Congress and JD(S) seats but not those of Siddaramaiah, D.K. Shivakumar or H.D. Kumaraswamy. Not every BJP-era grant was BJP-only.
- Deccan Herald, 19 Jul 2025, minister G. Parameshwara: "They (BJP) had given Rs 50 crore to their MLAs, while we (Congress MLAs) were given Rs 25 crore, Rs 20 crore, or even Rs 10 crore." No document cited (LOW on its own). No outlet found that cites a record for a "BJP 50 / Congress 25 under Bommai" rule; Q.114 is the official record that fits it.

## 4. Not the same everywhere
- The July 2021 Rs 1,277 crore release reportedly included Congress and JD(S) seats.
- Bengaluru Urban Development amounts varied widely within each party.

## What this does and does not show
It shows the pattern in sanctions on the record. It does not show money spent, anyone's personal gain, or why seats were chosen. It does not prove a uniform 50/25 rule for 2022, because the opposition side rests on two seats.
