## Notes for the owner before filing

- **Why item 5 asks for inspection:** measurement books for eleven packages run to thousands of pages. Asking for all of them invites a refusal under Section 7(9) (disproportionate diversion of resources). Inspection is a right under Section 2(j)(i) and lets you pick the pages that matter: the measurements of concrete laid against the length paid for.
- **Addresses:** confirm the current PIO for each office before posting. The Urban Development Department is at Vikasa Soudha; since the 2025 restructuring, road packages may be handled by the Greater Bengaluru Authority or by one of the five city corporations. Section 6(3) obliges a wrong office to forward it within five days.
- **Send** from a personal address, never a company mailbox. Keep scans of the signed applications and receipts in the private repo (`MLA-ARE-BADDDDD`, cases/), and log the filing dates in `ops/log/`.
- **Deadlines:** replies are due 30 days after receipt (35 if transferred). If there is no reply or a refusal, the first appeal goes to the First Appellate Authority (the officer senior to the PIO) within 30 days.
- **When replies arrive:** compare item B2 (paid and km done) with the Rs 160.7 crore in the public payment view and with street photos; compare A3 (released) with B2 (paid) to see whether money released by the state reached the packages.
