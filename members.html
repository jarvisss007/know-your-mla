MLCs and MPs

What Karnataka's 110 MLCs and MPs declared under oath when they stood for election: assets, debts and criminal cases. Every figure links to its source. We found an affidavit online for 85 of them.

These are declared figures only: no allegation, grade or analysis. Figures are as transcribed from the affidavits by the Association for Democratic Reforms on myneta.info. Assets and debts are what the member declared for themselves and their family on the date of filing; values are often book cost, not market value. If you see a mistake, tell us and we will correct it.
