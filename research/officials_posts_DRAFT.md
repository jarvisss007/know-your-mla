## 3. Case file: Excise Department (the best-documented chain in 2026)

- 17 Jan 2026: Lokayukta police arrested Excise Deputy Commissioner (Bengaluru Urban) Jagadeesh Naik, Superintendent K M Thammanna and constable Lakkappa Gani while allegedly accepting Rs 25 lakh. Complainant said Rs 80 lakh was first demanded for a CL-7 bar licence, and more for a microbrewery licence. Naik and Thammanna suspended 21 Jan 2026. (The News Minute, 5 Feb 2026; Deccan Herald)
- The complainant alleged the money was meant to reach "the minister and other officials". Opposition leader R. Ashoka alleged fixed rates per licence type and a Rs 5,000-6,000 crore scam; the Wine Merchants' Association made similar allegations. **Excise Minister R.B. Timmapur denied everything as false and political and refused to resign.** No agency finding names him in anything read so far.
- June 2026: ED searched 14 premises in a case concerning Additional Commissioner (Belagavi Division) Y D Manjunath. Reported seizures: Rs 5.5 crore cash, Rs 7.8 crore gold, foreign currency, and over 100 excise licences in other people's names. ED described middlemen collecting monthly payments from liquor outlets. (Free Press Journal, 26 Jun 2026; ED press release dated 25 Jun 2026, not yet read directly.)

## 4. Paying for a posting ("transfer business")

- Almost all of this is allegation by opposition politicians (H.D. Kumaraswamy's "transfer syndicate" claim, 2023; BJP claims in the PSI Parashuram death case, 2024). No conviction found.
- One documented action: 31 Aug 2026, three sub-inspectors in Uttara Kannada suspended after an audio clip about money allegedly paid for a posting at Haliyal station; a preliminary inquiry found prima facie misconduct. (The South First, 1 Sep 2026)
- Recruitment: PSI recruitment case (545 posts; CID chargesheet against a then-ADGP) and the 2026 KPSC veterinary officer recruitment case (an IAS officer arrested; CID said candidates paid Rs 40-80 lakh; High Court reportedly moved the probe to an SIT). Status of both needs checking.

## 6. What a citizen publication can actually do (accountability, not accusation)

1. **Track outcomes, not raids.** Build a public table: raid date, post, department, claimed value, then chargesheet / sanction / stay / verdict. The gap between column 1 and the last column is the story, and it is all from records.
2. **Sanction watch.** RTI to DPAR and each department: list of Lokayukta sanction requests pending, with dates received. Delay is a decision by a named authority.
3. **Property returns.** Officials must file annual property statements (Conduct Rules; Lokayukta Act s.22 for other public servants). The government has given the Assembly lists of IAS/KAS officers who did not file. Ask for the current list by RTI or through an MLA's question.
4. **Service-counter evidence.** Sub-registrar, RTO, taluk office, BESCOM: collect readers' written, dated experiences and forward them to the Lokayukta as complaints (Form I and II with affidavit). The owner files; we do not publish names from tips.
5. **Legislature questions.** Ask friendly MLAs/MLCs to put unstarred questions: department-wise cases, sanctions pending, convictions.
