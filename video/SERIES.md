# ಮಲ್ಲಣ್ಣ ಕೇಳ್ತಾನೆ · Mallanna Asks — Season 1: "ಕೆಲಸ ತೋರಿಸಿ" (Show Me the Work)

A Shorts series (40-60 s each, Kannada first, English line under every scene) on the Gandaberunda channel.
Fictional characters, real public records. Every number on screen carries its source.

## The world

**Mallanna** (45), runs a small tea stall by the bus stand in a mid-sized north Karnataka town. Pays every tax there is, never asks for anything. Calm, dry humour. Catchphrase: **ಕೆಲಸ ತೋರಿಸಿ, ಆಮೇಲೆ ಮತ** (Show the work, then the vote).

**Kaveri** (12), his daughter, in a government school. Bright, asks "why?" about everything. The series' heart: every episode ends up being about her future.

**Amma** (70), Mallanna's mother. Diabetic, needs the government hospital.

**Raju**, Mallanna's friend, a small civil contractor who has not been paid for a road he finished.

**The Neta** (no name, no party, no colours). Appears only at election time, always smiling, always with a garland, a gift bag and a promise. Each episode he has a new excuse. He is a type, never a real person.

**The rule of the show:** the characters are invented; the money is real. Real politicians appear only as names in public records, with what the record says, attributed. No unproven crimes, no insults, both parties.

## The arc

Season 1 runs from today to the 2028 Assembly election. Each episode is one bill Mallanna pays and one thing that did not come back. Mallanna goes from quietly paying, to asking, to getting his street to ask, to the finale: a town meeting where the Neta arrives and nobody takes the gift bag.

Running gags: the Neta's garland gets bigger every episode (his declared wealth); Mallanna's tea price is the only price in town that hasn't gone up; Kaveri keeps a notebook titled "Questions for the MLA".

## Format of every episode

1. Cold open (0-4 s): a line from the Neta that everyone has heard.
2. Mallanna's day (the relatable cost).
3. The record: 2-3 numbers, big, sourced.
4. Fairness beat: what improved, or which other government did the same.
5. Mallanna's question + Kaveri's notebook line.
6. Call to action: "What work has your MLA shown you?" + site link.

## Guardrails

- Allegations are attributed by name and date; where no court or auditor has found wrongdoing, the video says so.
- No real person's family member is mocked; adults in public roles appear only through what they publicly said or did, on the record.
- Children in the story are fictional.
- Every script's figures are re-checked against the source before rendering; corrections go in the description and the repo.
