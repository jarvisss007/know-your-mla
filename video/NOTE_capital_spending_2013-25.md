## World Bank and the deficit. MEDIUM: from search results, pages not opened

- Loans from the Government of India (the head that carries externally aided project loans) were 6 to 8 per cent of public debt receipts in 2013-18 (table above, HIGH).
- World Bank press release, 24 June 2025: USD 426 million programme for Bengaluru water security (Congress government).
- A USD 363 million World Bank rural water programme was reported approved in March 2023 (BJP government).
- Revenue deficit of Rs 9,271 crore in 2023-24, reported from the CAG audit by Outlook; PRS gives a revised estimate of Rs 25,449 crore for 2025-26. The state was in revenue surplus every year from 2013-14 to 2017-18 (HIGH).

## What this does and does not show

Capital spending's share rose in the last two years of the 2013-18 Congress government, stayed between 17 and 21 per cent through the BJP years, and fell in the first two years of the present Congress government. It does not show that either party builds more as a rule, and no audit finding read here says who gains from capital works.
