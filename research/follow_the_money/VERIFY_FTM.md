The method follows research/fundflow/VERIFY_PART4.md. The page's 93 numeric statements (about 229 figures) were each checked against the cited document, on the printed page. Scanned answers were read as page images. Our own sums were recomputed from the source tables or from the repo data files.
- Documents: CAG SFAR 2024-25 (Report 2 of 2026), SFAR 2023-24 (Report 4 of 2025), KRIDL performance audit (Report 9 of 2022), Compliance Audit Report 7 of 2024, SPSE Report 1 of 2025, PMKKKY/DMF performance audit (Report 7 of 2025), Yettinahole performance audit (Report 10 of 2025), Compliance Report 1 of 2026 (chapters I and II), and Assembly answers Q.401 (9 Dec 2025) and Q.3223 (24 Mar 2026).
- Statuses: VERIFIED = number and wording on the cited page. CORRECTED = the source gives a different number, scope or wording; the page is now fixed. DISPUTED = the figure is right, but the report prints a Government reply contesting it; the reply is now on the page. NOT CHECKED = the source is a press report or an earlier audit not re-read in this pass; the page attributes it as such.
