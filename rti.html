Gandaberunda · Right to Information · for any citizen of India

Ask for the answer yourself

64 Karnataka officials were raided, trapped or charged in 2026, according to the reports we track. For 55 of them, no later step has been reported: no chargesheet, no sanction, no verdict. The offices that know the answer must reply to any citizen within 30 days. Here are four letters, ready to print, sign and post.

See every case on After the raid. Letters carry posts and places, not names, the same as the tracker; the PIO can identify each officer from the post, place, date and the linked press report.

The letters

1. Lokayukta police: what happened to the 2026 cases

The Lokayukta police know whether each case has a chargesheet, a pending sanction request or a court stay. Part A asks only for totals, which should be answered even if Part B is refused.

The annex lists all 57 officers the Lokayukta acted against in the tracker, by post, place and date.

2. Government: sanction requests left pending

A case cannot go to trial until the officer's department gives sanction to prosecute. In 2024 the Lokayukta said 206 officials' sanctions had been pending for months. This asks for today's list, by department and designation, and the rules on time limits.

3. One department: suspension, sanction, promotion

Each department decides whether its officer is suspended, whether to allow prosecution, and whether to transfer or promote them. Suspension and posting orders are routine office orders. Pick a department below; its officers are added as the annex.

4. Officers who never declared their property

Every government servant must file an annual statement of property. A missing return is often the first sign investigators look for. The government has given such lists to the Assembly before.

How to file

  1. Fill and sign. Copy or download a letter, print it, fill the blanks (postal order number, place, date) and sign. For letter 3, add the department's head-office address from its website.
  2. Pay Rs 10. Attach an Indian Postal Order (or demand draft) for Rs 10, payable to the office's accounting officer as named on its RTI page. Applicants below the poverty line attach a copy of their BPL card instead. Copying charges are a few rupees a page; check the office's RTI page, since guides differ.
  3. Post it by Speed Post or registered post with acknowledgement. Keep a copy, the postal receipt and the postal-order counterfoil. Some departments also accept online RTI; check the department's site or the Karnataka Information Commission.
  4. Wait 30 days. The reply is due within 30 days of receipt. If your letter is sent on to another office, that office has its own 30 days.
  5. No reply, or refused? File a first appeal within 30 days to the First Appellate Authority (usually named in the reply or on the office's RTI page). A second appeal goes to the Karnataka Information Commission within 90 days.
  6. Tell us the answer. Send us what you received and we will add it to the tracker, with the post and the step, never your name.

If the office refuses

  • "Ongoing investigation" (Section 8(1)(h)). The office must explain how answering would hamper the investigation; quoting the section is not enough. Dates of a chargesheet or a sanction request reveal no evidence.
  • "Personal information" (Section 8(1)(j)). The raids were announced by the Lokayukta and reported in the press. Ask for the information with names removed rather than refused (Section 10). Part A of letter 1 asks only for totals.
  • "Exempt from RTI" (Section 24). In 2026 the Supreme Court held that Madhya Pradesh's Lokayukta police is not an intelligence or security organisation that can be exempted. If an office cites an exemption notification, ask for a copy.

Please

  • Ask for records, not opinions, and keep to the questions. A short, specific letter gets a better answer.
  • Do not add anyone's private details, and do not accuse anyone in the letter. A raid is an allegation, not a finding of guilt.
  • The fee and filing route are as described by RTI guides and offices' own pages as of Nov 2026; check them before you post.
