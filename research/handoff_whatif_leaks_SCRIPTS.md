# HANDOFF to video session: four ready-to-film Shorts (What if? and the leaks)

Prepared by the research session, 18 Nov 2026. Every figure below was checked against the CAG report text. Pages on the site: `money.html` (What if?) and `reports/follow_the_money.html` (Follow the rupee).
Kannada lines are research-session drafts: have the Kannada reader check them before they go on screen.

**Rules for all four**
- No person is named. These are about how the system works, under both parties.
- Never "looted", "stolen", "scam". Use the auditor's words: parked, stuck, not recovered, not matched.
- On screen, show the report and paragraph, e.g. "CAG Report No. 2 of 2026, para 3.5".
- End card: "Try it yourself: jarvisss007.github.io/know-your-mla/money.html"
- Length 35-45 seconds. Characters as in the Mallanna series (Mallanna asks, the official answers). Format per `video/talk.py`.

Total Rs 9,367 cr. 9,367 crore / 14.5 lakh = 64,600; / 4.8 crore = 1,951.
