Rural job scheme (MGNREGA) · Karnataka · records of 2024-25

The government's own photos. Watch them.

Every finished work under the rural job scheme needs a photo as proof. These short films are cut only from those photos and the records beside them. Nothing is staged or generated. Each film is about one minute, in Kannada with English lines on screen.

Kampli · Ballari

152 works on record. One tank in the photos.

Canals, ponds and bunds from 7 panchayats, with ₹5.91 crore recorded against them. All 152 completion photos were taken within about 400 metres of one point.

Karatagi and Gangavathi · Koppal

Six check dams. One bush.

The Jeerala tank carries 94 works from 11 panchayats and ₹7.14 crore. Six photos recorded as separate check dams were taken within ten minutes at the same spot.

Kushtagi · Koppal

Three works. One cement tank.

The accepted photos of three separate works show the same pipe, the same rag and the same stones. Found by comparing 23,580 completion photos with one another.

The findingsAll 236 blocks ranked

What these films do and do not say

They show what the public record shows. They do not say any person did anything wrong: wages under the scheme are paid into workers' own bank accounts, and real digging was done at both tanks. The open question is whether the money bought many works or one work counted many times, and only the measurement books can answer it.
