# Mallanna Asks: theme (lyrics, original)

Short chant for the start and end of every episode (about 15 seconds). Simple folk rhythm, clap-along; works with a tamate or dollu beat. Record it yourself, with a local singer, or with a music app on your own account. Keep the master in this repo.

## Kannada (main)

> ಐದು ವರ್ಷಕ್ಕೊಮ್ಮೆ ಬರ್ತಾರೆ, ಕೈ ಮುಗಿದು ನಗ್ತಾರೆ,
> ಸೀರೆ ಕುಕ್ಕರ್ ಕೊಡ್ತಾರೆ, ಲೆಕ್ಕ ಮಾತ್ರ ಮರೀತಾರೆ.
>
> **ಕೆಲಸ ತೋರಿಸಿ ಸ್ವಾಮಿ, ಕೆಲಸ ತೋರಿಸಿ!**
> **ಆಮೇಲೆ ಮತ, ಮೊದಲು ಲೆಕ್ಕ ಕೊಡಿ!**
>
> ಹಾಲು ಏರಿತು, ಬಸ್ಸು ಏರಿತು, ನೀರು ಮಾತ್ರ ಬರಲಿಲ್ಲ,
> ಮಲ್ಲಣ್ಣ ಕೇಳ್ತಾನೆ, ಕಾವೇರಿ ಬರೀತಾಳೆ, ಉತ್ತರ ಇನ್ನೂ ಬರಲಿಲ್ಲ.
>
> **ಕೆಲಸ ತೋರಿಸಿ ಸ್ವಾಮಿ, ಕೆಲಸ ತೋರಿಸಿ!**

## English (for the English cuts)

> Once in five years, at the door with a smile,
> a sari, a cooker, and a promise for a while.
>
> **Show me the work, sir, show me the work!**
> **Then you get my vote: first show the books!**
>
> Milk went up, the bus went up, the water never came,
> Mallanna keeps asking, Kaveri writes it down, the answer's still the same.
>
> **Show me the work, sir, show me the work!**

## Sound brief for a singer or music app

Upbeat Kannada folk / street-theatre feel, 100-110 BPM, male lead with a group answering the hook, dollu or tamate percussion, harmonium. No resemblance to any existing film song.
