Prepared by the research session, 17 Nov 2026. Both are live on the site: `index.html#promised` and `money.html`.
Confidence: **HIGH** = read in the CAG report text (checked line by line against the report). **MEDIUM** = from a named news report quoting an official, or a cost worked out from official totals.

**On-screen source rule:** show the report name and paragraph (e.g. "CAG Report No. 2 of 2026, para 3.5"), not URLs.

**Never say:** "looted", "stolen", "scam" for any audit figure. Money "parked", "stuck", "not matched" or "not recovered" is what the auditor found; it is not proof of theft by anyone. Do not name any person in a Short built from this file.

## 1. How much each government spent on building (HIGH)

Source: CAG State Finances Audit Report 2024-25 (Report No. 2 of 2026), ten-year tables. Share = our arithmetic from the same table.

| Year | Government | Capital spending (Rs crore) | Share of all spending |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2019-20 | BJP (budget drawn up by the previous government) | 35,530 | 16.6% |
| 2020-21 | BJP (first Covid year) | 45,406 | 20.3% |
| 2021-22 | BJP | 47,874 | 18.3% |
| 2022-23 | BJP | 57,349 | 20.8% |
| 2023-24 | Congress | 52,120 | 17.4% |
| 2024-25 | Congress | 57,906 | 16.9% |

- In 2023-24, "the borrowings exceeded the Capital Expenditure by 38,160 crore and the amount was utilised for meeting the expenses towards five guarantee schemes." (CAG Report No. 4 of 2025, Executive Summary) **HIGH**
- The five guarantees cost Rs 52,525.60 crore in 2024-25, 19% of the state's day-to-day spending. (Report No. 2 of 2026) **HIGH**
- Fair line to add: the auditor also credits the schemes. Shakti "have given mobility to women"; Gruha Jyothi "helped households save on monthly electricity bills". (Report No. 2 of 2026, Chapter 1) **HIGH**

## 2. Missed by both governments (HIGH unless marked)

- **Suburban rail.** June 2022: the Prime Minister promised it in 40 months. The CAG says two of four corridors "have not taken off" (Report No. 2 of 2026, appendix on budget assurances). The rail company now targets December 2028 for the first line and March 2030 for all of it (MEDIUM, Deccan Herald).
- **Yettinahole.** Approved at Rs 8,323.50 crore (2012); now Rs 23,251.66 crore. Rs 15,297 crore spent by March 2024. Works done "in a disjointed manner without achieving the Project objective even after 12 years of conception". Rs 181.21 crore of "excess benefit to the contractors". (CAG Report No. 10) Water was first lifted in September 2024; no source reports any reaching Kolar or Chikkaballapura (MEDIUM).
- **Upper Bhadra.** Rs 5,300 crore set aside in the Union Budget of February 2023; not released as of August 2026 (MEDIUM, ThePrint). The Centre told Parliament the project is under no central scheme (Rajya Sabha reply, 10 Feb 2025).

## 3. What each delivered (one each side, minimum)

- BJP: Shivamogga airport, inaugurated 27 February 2023, commercial flights from 31 August 2023 (MEDIUM, Business Standard). Capital spending above budget in 2021-22 and 2022-23 (HIGH, CAG).
- Congress: free bus travel for women, Rs 5,615 crore in 2024-25; free power, Rs 9,157.66 crore in 2024-25 (HIGH, CAG). Metro Yellow Line opened August 2025, a joint Centre-state project the BJP government had promised for December 2021 (MEDIUM).

## 4. What if? (the simulator, money.html)

The money the auditor found stuck, unrecovered or unaccounted for (all HIGH, CAG Reports No. 2 of 2026, No. 10, No. 13 of 2025):

| Pot | Rs crore | Auditor's word |
|---|---|---|
| Parked in deposit accounts (leaving out Rs 22,115 cr held under a Supreme Court order) | 7,655 | stuck |
| Works payments not matched between division offices and treasury | 965 | "could lead to non-detection of cases of defalcation" |
| Shown as spent, still in welfare corporations' banks | 412 | stuck |
| Excess benefit to Yettinahole contractors | 181 | not recovered |
| Rural jobs scheme misappropriation closed without recovery | 90 | "settled without any recovery" |
| Advance bills still without receipts | 64 | "fraught with the risk of misappropriation" |
| **Total** | **9,367** | |

With the simulator's default mix, Rs 9,367 crore buys, at official unit costs (MEDIUM; costs from budget documents and ministers' statements, listed on the page):
14,908 classrooms (Rs 14.5 lakh each) · 300 primary health centres · 20 taluk hospitals · 1,978 km of village road rebuilt · 74,805 tap connections · 6,005 anganwadis · 60,045 rural homes · 1,901 buses · 1,275 check dams.
Say "could have built", and say the mix is a choice. Each item alone: Rs 9,367 crore = about 64,600 classrooms, or about 1,950 primary health centres.

**California line (MEDIUM, our arithmetic):** California's economy is about 11.6 times Karnataka's and about 20 times richer per person (US BEA 2025; Karnataka Economic Survey 2025-26; Rs 88.37 to the dollar). If income per person grows 7% a year in Karnataka and 2% in California, it takes about 63 years to catch up. Present it as "how far we have to go", never as a prediction.

## Short ideas (scripts to be written by the video session)

1. **"Rs 9,367 crore"**: Mallanna counts what the auditor found parked and unrecovered, then shows the classrooms and clinics it could have been. Ends: "Ask your MLA where your district's share went." Link: money.html.
2. **"Both promised. Neither delivered."**: suburban rail, Yettinahole, Upper Bhadra; one government's promise, the next government's delay, same people waiting.
3. **"The last day of March"**: Rs 10,093 crore spent on 31 March 2025 alone (CAG Report No. 2 of 2026, para 2.5.8, HIGH). Why a rush to spend before the year ends is a warning sign.
4. **"63 years"**: the California comparison, framed as a challenge: what would have to change to make it 25.
