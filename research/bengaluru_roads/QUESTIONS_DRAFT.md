## 1. Email to OpenCity

To: the contact address on data.opencity.in (owner to confirm the current address on the site's About or Contact page)
Subject: Question about the BBMP work orders and payments datasets, 2024-25 and 2025-26

Hello,

Thank you for publishing the BBMP work orders and payments datasets. We are using the 2024-25 and 2025-26 files for a citizen check of road works in Bengaluru.

We noticed that some bill IDs appear twice: once with a payment (RTGS) reference from April to November 2024, and again with a new reference from November 2024 onwards. Everything else (bill register number, bill date, gross and net amount) is identical. For example, bill 613688 (White Topping, Phase 2, Package 2, Rs 11,49,24,150) appears with RTGS 005087 dated 22 Aug 2024 and again with RTGS 000095 dated 16 Jul 2025. About 2,900 bills follow this pattern, almost all first listed between April and November 2024, around the point where the RTGS numbering restarts.

Could you tell us:
1. When each of the two files was collected from accounts.bbmp.gov.in, and whether the collection method changed between them?
2. Whether you have seen the public view list the same bill again when its payment is re-issued or re-recorded, for example after a change in the payment system in late 2024?

We want to describe the data correctly and not mistake a records change for a double payment. Any pointers would help.

With thanks,
[owner's name], Gandaberunda (independent citizen publication, Karnataka)
