## Thu 19 Nov, 8:30 PM IST: THREAD "Follow the rupee" (7 posts)

1/ How does a rupee in Karnataka's budget reach a road, a worker or a pension, and where does it stop on the way? We followed it through seven stages using the state auditor's own reports. A thread. 🧵 FTR

2/ Stage 1: the legislature votes it. In 2024-25, ₹4,389 crore was spent above what the legislature approved, and ₹10,448 crore of unspent money was handed back on the last day of the year. (CAG State Finances Audit 2024-25)

3/ Stage 2: year-end parking. ₹6,026 crore went into deposit accounts in March 2025 alone. The auditor's phrase for one such transfer: "mere parking of funds to avoid lapse of grants".

4/ Ten development corporations kept 32 bank accounts open after a 2023 order to close them: ₹410 crore on 31 March 2025. Four corporations held ₹412 crore of grants the books showed as spent.

5/ Stage 4: works. KRIDL split 12 works worth ₹92 crore into 85 to stay under the tender-exemption limit; the auditor found departments "preferred KRIDL to bypass the tender process".

6/ Mining money: district mineral funds collected ₹4,004 crore by 2023 and spent ₹1,826 crore. The CM-led committee meant to oversee them did not meet once from 2018 to June 2023.

7/ Every stage leaves a record any citizen can ask for. Six ready-to-post RTI letters, in Kannada and English, ₹10 each: RTI
