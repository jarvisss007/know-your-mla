# ರೈತನ ಹಕ್ಕು · The Farmer's Due: Shorts scripts (DRAFT)

**Research session to video session handoff.** Eight Shorts of 40-50 seconds each, Kannada first, with an English line under every scene. Source on screen for every number. Built from `SOURCES_DRAFT.md` (facts as of 16 Nov 2026).

**Before rendering:**
- Episodes marked **Official** use figures read in a PIB, Lok Sabha or guideline document and can go to render after the usual re-check.
- Episodes marked **Press** need the second, independent verification pass from CHANNEL_OPS step 3 first.
- A native Kannada speaker should read every line. Claude wrote the Kannada.
- Krishi Bhagya, Ganga Kalyana and Raitha Vidya Nidhi are left out until their amounts are confirmed in India.

## The world

**Basanna** (52) is Mallanna's elder brother. He farms 3 acres of rainfed land outside the town, grows ragi and some navane, and keeps two cows. He's proud and tired, and he believes "schemes are for people who know someone." He is fictional.

**Kaveri** (12) visits him in the holidays. She has the scheme finder on her father's phone and reads it out to him. Each episode she finds one thing he is owed and he didn't know about.

**Fairness rule:** every episode says which government runs the scheme. Central schemes are named as central and state schemes as state. Where payments were late, the episode says under which government and when, as recorded.

**Ending of every episode:** Kaveri says «ಹಕ್ಕು ಕೇಳಿ, ಭಿಕ್ಷೆ ಅಲ್ಲ» ("Ask for what's yours. It isn't charity."), then the card "Check what you qualify for → link in description" and the finder link.
