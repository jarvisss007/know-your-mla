## Court
WP 31626/2025 with WP 28664/2025 (Prakash Belawadi and Dr Adikesavalu Ravindra v. State) and connected petitions challenge the tender, the feasibility study, the DPR and the environmental exemption. Last proceeding found: about 3 Sep 2026 (GSI report called for), listed 10 Sep 2026. Nothing found after that.

## The government's case
Shivakumar: skips 25 bottlenecks; 90 minutes to 45 (DPR: 35); "the only option". B-SMILE: "completely data-driven". Priyank Kharge: "We have not influenced the tender process."

## Cost per km (Rs crore)
Tunnel, tender estimate about 1,057; Adani bid about 1,330; Namma Metro Phase 3 (elevated) about 350; Mumbai Coastal Road about 1,322-1,415. The tender figure is a concession value (includes maintenance and returns), so not like-for-like with metro build costs.
