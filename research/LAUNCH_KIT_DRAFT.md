## 3. Note to Kannada journalists and local reporters

Subject: Free data: report cards for all 224 Karnataka MLAs, with district records

> Namaskara. Gandaberunda is an independent, citizen-run site that puts public records about Karnataka's MLAs in one place. Every MLA now has a report-card page (https://jarvisss007.github.io/know-your-mla/mla/) with their declared assets, debts and criminal cases from their 2023 affidavit, wealth growth since 2018 for re-elected MLAs, and their district's 2023 drought declaration and crop-insurance claims (Lok Sabha, 3 Feb 2026). Everything links to its source and is free to use, with or without credit. The full dataset is at https://github.com/jarvisss007/know-your-mla/blob/main/data/mlas.csv. We would welcome corrections. Readers can send us tips through the site, and we pass verifiable leads to reporters who want them.
