# DRAFT reader request: photos of three Bengaluru roads

Status: wording APPROVED by the owner on 17 Nov 2026 (English and X post). The Kannada still needs a native reader before use.
Where it would go: the Have your say page (owned by the research and site session), X and WhatsApp (the owner posts).

## Why photos, and why Mapillary

The Google Form behind Have your say cannot take photo uploads without a sign-in. Mapillary's free phone app can. It records the date and place automatically, removes faces and number plates, and publishes under an open licence. Our script (`street_photos.py`) then finds the new photos with no further work from the reader. Readers who prefer not to use an app can still tell us through the form what they saw.

## English (site / WhatsApp)

**Help us check three roads in Bengaluru**

The city corporation's own payment records show money paid between 2023 and 2025 to put a concrete top layer ("white-topping") on these roads:

| Road | Stretch named in the work order | Paid (gross) | Bills dated |
|---|---|---|---|
| Nagarabhavi 80 Feet Road | Outer Ring Road to Kengunte Circle | Rs 15.45 crore | Mar 2023 to Jul 2025 |
| Jawaharlal Nehru Road, RR Nagar | Uttarahalli Road to Jayanna Circle | Rs 20.49 crore | Nov 2023 to May 2025 |
| Taralabalu Road, RT Nagar | Bellary Road to Rabindranath Tagore Circle, and RT Nagar Main Road to Citizen Park | Rs 24.03 crore | Dec 2023 to Jul 2025 |

Satellite pictures are too coarse to tell concrete from tar on these streets, and we found no recent street photos online. If you pass by, a few photos settle it.

How to help:
1. Install the free **Mapillary** app (Android or iPhone). Sign up with any email.
2. Walk or ride along the stretch in daylight and take photos every few metres, phone pointed along the road. The app saves the date and place and blurs faces and number plates.
3. Upload in the app. That's all: we find the photos automatically.

No app? Use Have your say and tell us the road, the date you passed, and what the surface is: concrete (light grey, with cut lines across it), tar (black), or a mix.

Please stay safe. Take photos from the footpath or as a passenger, never while driving.

We are not saying anything was or was not done. We are checking that what was paid for is on the road, the same way for every ward and every party. Source: BBMP work-order and payment records published by OpenCity (data.opencity.in), compiled from accounts.bbmp.gov.in.
