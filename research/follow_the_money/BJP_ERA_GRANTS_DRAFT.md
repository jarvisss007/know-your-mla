# DRAFT: the same split under the BJP government, 2022 (25 Nov 2026)

Companion to CM_GRANTS_BY_SEAT_DRAFT.md. The main list was read twice independently (the second blind); the party of every seat on the date of the answer was rebuilt from the 2018 results and every change to February 2022.

## Main source
Legislative Assembly unstarred question 114, asked by Mahadeva K. (Periyapatna, JD(S)), answered and signed by Chief Minister Basavaraj Bommai on 15 Feb 2022: https://kla.kar.nic.in/assembly/answers/pdf/15022022/114.pdf
- Part (c), whether Rs 3,322.12 crore of special grant was given: "Yes; details are in the annexure".
- Part (d), whether the disparity will be corrected and all constituencies given the grant: "ಉದ್ಭವಿಸುವುದಿಲ್ಲ" ("Does not arise").
- Annexure: 233 rows across 12 departments, 64 constituencies; every printed department total and the grand total (3,322.12) match.

## Findings
- 63 of the 64 seats got exactly Rs 52 crore (Rs 50 crore of works plus Rs 2 crore for temples); Gundlupet got Rs 46.12 crore.
- On 15 Feb 2022 the 64 seats were held by 63 BJP members and 1 independent who backed the government (Mulbagal; he joined Congress only in Jan 2023). No seat held by Congress (69 seats then) or JD(S) (32) is in the list.
- Six of the listed seats were BJP-held because of 2019-21 changes: Athani, Kagwad and Ranibennur (Dec 2019 by-elections), Sira (Nov 2020), Basavakalyan (Apr 2021), Sindgi (Oct 2021).
- The same grant had reached Rs 4,518.12 crore across 15 departments by 29 Mar 2022 (USQ 4668; no per-seat list).
- Opposition seats got Rs 25 crore later: Yemkanamardi (Congress), orders of 9-10 May 2022 (USQ 297, answered 20 Dec 2022); Varuna (Congress), order of 10 May 2022 (stated in USQ 1097, answered 21 Feb 2023). Bhadravathi (Congress) asked the Rural Development department for Rs 50 crore and was refused for lack of funds (USQ 1635, 23 Feb 2022).

## Earlier years (news only, not documents)
- Yediyurappa government, 2019-21: Deccan Herald reported Rs 427 crore to 79 BJP MLAs (up to Rs 25 crore each) and cuts to Congress and JD(S) seats; the government froze Rs 642.5 crore of grants sanctioned under the coalition (USQ 1791, Sep 2019 freeze). Articles not opened (403); search summaries only.
- Congress-JD(S) coalition, 2018-19: no per-seat data either way.
- In July 2025, then minister G. Parameshwara said the BJP had given its MLAs Rs 50 crore and Congress MLAs Rs 25, 20 or 10 crore (PTI via The Week); the documents bear out the 50 vs 25.
