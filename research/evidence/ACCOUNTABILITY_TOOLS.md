# Accountability tools: what the law says (checked 23 Nov 2026)

Source notes for the "What you can do" steps on the Evidence page (`tools/make_evidence_page.py`). Karnataka government sites were mostly unreachable from outside India, so some items rest on gazette copies or secondary summaries; each says which.

## RTI (Right to Information Act, 2005)
- Reply within 30 days; within 48 hours where life or liberty is concerned (s.7(1)). No fee for people below the poverty line (s.7(5)).
- First appeal within 30 days to the officer senior in rank (s.19(1)). Second appeal within 90 days to the Karnataka Information Commission (s.19(3)).
- Penalty on the PIO: Rs 250 a day, capped at Rs 25,000 (s.20(1)).
- Source: the Act as on 18 Nov 2025, https://cic.gov.in/sites/default/files/RTI_English.pdf
- Karnataka RTI Rules (secondary, CHRI summary): Rs 10 application fee by postal order, demand draft, bankers' cheque or treasury remittance. Copies Rs 2 a page. One subject and at most 150 words per application (2008 amendment).

## Karnataka Lokayukta Act, 1984
- Any person may complain (s.9(1)). The complaint must be supported by an affidavit, in the prescribed form (s.9(2)).
- Rules (snippet): Form I is the complaint and Form II the affidavit, sworn before a magistrate, notary, oath commissioner or gazetted officer. File in person or by registered post.
- A false complaint is punishable with 6 months to 3 years and a fine (s.20(1)).
- Source: consolidated Act, https://prsindia.org/files/bills_acts/acts_states/karnataka/1985/1985KR4.pdf
- The ACB was quashed by the Karnataka HC on 11 Aug 2022 and its cases moved to the Lokayukta (secondary reports).

## Prevention of Corruption Act, 1988, as amended in 2018
- s.19 proviso: the authority shall "endeavour to convey the decision ... within a period of three months", extendable by one month for legal consultation. The Supreme Court held the limit binding: once it lapses, the aggrieved party may approach the writ court. Source: Vijay Rajmohan v. State, 11 Oct 2022, https://api.sci.gov.in/supremecourt/2022/3264/3264_2022_15_1501_38812_Judgement_11-Oct-2022.pdf
- s.17A (prior approval before inquiring into an official decision): split verdict on 13 Jan 2026 (CPIL v. Union of India, 2026 INSC 55), referred to a larger bench. It still operates for now. Not stated on the page.
- s.8 proviso (snippet): a person compelled to pay a bribe is protected if they report it within 7 days.
- Vineet Narain (1998) 1 SCC 226 set the 3-month limit; Subramanian Swamy v. Manmohan Singh (2012) 3 SCC 64 held it binding. "Deemed sanction" is not law.

## Rural jobs
- MGNREGA stands repealed from 1 Jul 2026. It is replaced by the Viksit Bharat - Guarantee for Rozgar and Ajeevika Mission (Gramin) Act, 2025, which keeps "mandatory social audits ... and grievance redressal" (PIB, May 2026). Section numbers not found.
- Karnataka's social audit body is the Directorate of Social Audit (CAG Report 13 of 2025). The 2024-25 photo findings on the site concern MGNREGA works and stand as records of that scheme.

## Legislature
- Unstarred questions get written answers, with no supplementaries. The PAC examines CAG reports. Citizens have no formal route: ask an MLA or MLC to give notice. Source: Council review 2013-18, https://kla.kar.nic.in/council/Research/RandR/klcreview2013_18.pdf
