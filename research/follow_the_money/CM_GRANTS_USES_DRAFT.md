# DRAFT: what the CM's grants are spent on, and the fuller August 2025 list (25 Nov 2026)

Single read of scanned Assembly answers; every seat row adds to its printed seat total and the grand totals match. Allocations, not money spent. The department each seat's money went to is used as the use category (no answer lists every funded work by name).

## Sources
- **USQ 1793, answered 19 Aug 2025** (https://kla.kar.nic.in/assembly/answers/16assembly/pdf/19082025/1793.pdf):
  - pp. 2-5: CM's special grant 2023-24, 140 rows, each seat's money split across 25 departments, Rs 3,510 crore (printed department totals match after the Badami row, typed in lakh, is converted).
  - pp. 6-12: rain grant, Rs 10 crore each to 189 seats (equal by design).
  - pp. 13-18: CM's infrastructure programme (CMIDP) 2025-26, 223 seats split across 30 departments, Rs 9,114 crore.
- **USQ 2844, 18 Mar 2026**: p. 5 splits the Rural Development money by executing agency; p. 39 lists the 2024-25 CM special sanctions with order dates.
- **USQ 114, 15 Feb 2022** (BJP era): department split per seat.
- **USQ 970**: Nanjangud's Backward Classes Welfare share was 43 community halls and hostels of about Rs 5 lakh each (supports reading BCW money as halls).

## Timing: the 2024 by-elections
The 2024-25 CM special list in USQ 2844 (Rs 113.8 crore) gave Rs 78.8 crore (69%) to the three seats going to the polls, all ordered before the vote:
- Shiggaon: Rs 30 crore, 244 works through KRIDL, ordered 5 Oct 2024 (10 days before the poll was announced, 39 days before voting).
- Channapatna: Rs 39.8 crore, ordered between 14 Aug and 30 Sep 2024.
- Sandur: Rs 9 crore, ordered 16 May 2024.
(These are the Rural Development portions with order dates; the larger two-year totals in USQ 1119 - Rs 150, 125 and 100 crore - have no dates. Page 5 of USQ 2844 says 2024-25 covered 33 seats and Rs 210 crore, but p. 39 shows only 5 seats: not explained.)

## Who carries out the works
KRIDL, a state company that usually gets work by nomination under a tender-exemption clause, executed about half of the Rural Development portion: 46.5% in 2023-24, 63.7% in 2024-25, 52.3% of the rain works, 28.5% of CMIDP 2025-26 - about Rs 1,861 crore of Rs 3,820 crore. The answers do not say whether these works were tendered. No contractor is named anywhere.

## What this does and does not show
- Most of the money goes to roads; temples and community halls are a small but visible share (5-17% together under the Congress lists, about 5% under the BJP list).
- The by-election money was ordered in the weeks before the 2024 vote. Whether that broke the model code of conduct depends on dates the answers give only for the Rural Development portion; the code took effect on announcement (15 Oct 2024), after all three orders.
- Not shown: what was built, or anyone's personal gain.
