# District research: DRAFT, NOT YET VERIFIED

These files are first-pass research notes on public money in Karnataka's 31 districts since about 2000:
projects that ran late or over cost, unspent funds, and outcome trends. Every item carries the
source URL and an exact quote, but the numbers have NOT yet been through an independent re-check.
Items marked "medium" confidence need checking against the primary document.

Nothing here is published on the site until it passes that check.
Errors or better sources: open an issue.
