# Karnataka irrigation money trail — public-record findings (compiled 20 Nov 2026)

**How to read this.** Every bullet is from a document I opened unless marked otherwise. CAG and Lok Sabha figures are from text PDFs. Karnataka Legislative Assembly (KLA) answers are scanned Kannada pages I read as images, so re-check each KLA figure against the PDF before print. "Derived" means my own arithmetic on printed figures. All amounts are Rs crore unless stated.

**Two things in your brief that the records qualify:**
- The Rs 14,155 crore off-budget borrowing (31 Mar 2025) is not "mostly" the four Nigams. They hold Rs 7,278.11 crore (51%, derived); Rs 5,257.70 crore is Power Company of Karnataka Ltd.
- The "seven contractors, 67%" finding is 67% of 43 works by number (29 works). By value those 29 works are 88% of the 43 works and 66.7% of all 55 Yettinahole contracts (derived).
