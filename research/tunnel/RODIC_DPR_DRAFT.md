## 2. The DPR contract (BBMP, Hebbal–Silk Board tunnel road)

**Tender and award**
- BBMP called for bids to prepare the DPR for the 18-km Hebbal (Esteem Mall)–Silk Board tunnel road. **BBMP's own estimate was Rs 15 crore, which it described as about 0.1% of the projected project cost.** Two bidders took part. BBMP rejected the second bid because the bidder was **Altinok Consulting Engineering Inc, which BBMP had already hired for the feasibility report on the same project.** That left Rodic Consultants as the lone bidder, and Rodic won at **Rs 9.45 crore**. Chief Commissioner Tushar Girinath: "Not many consultants participated in the tender"; he said the winning quote was below BBMP's estimate. No source reached gives the bid amounts. The rejected bid's value was not disclosed. — NewsKarnataka, 4 Sep 2024, https://newskarnataka.com/bengaluru/bbmp-to-spend/04092024/ [REC]; Deccan Herald, Sept 2024, https://www.deccanherald.com/india/karnataka/bengaluru/tunnel-road-project-bbmp-to-spend-rs-9-45-cr-on-dpr-alone-3175720 [SNIP]
- The tender date, KPPP number and published scope were **not found**. KPPP is not indexed, and the OpenCity tender PDFs sit behind Cloudflare.
- Contract value as reported: Rs 9.45 cr (NewsKarnataka/DH); "Rs 90.5 million" (Rs 9.05 cr; ConstructionWorld, 10 Jan 2025); "Rs 95 million received" (ConstructionWorld, 21 May 2025); "Rs 9.5 cr" (Surya; DH). **[INF]** These most likely describe the same contract, with or without GST or rounding. No primary work order was seen.
- Speed: the DPR was produced in about 3 months, against an industry norm of 9–12 months. The draft is dated Sept 2024 (executive summary 24.09.2024) and was submitted about Nov 2024. — DH "DPR out in just 3 months", https://www.deccanherald.com/india/karnataka/bengaluru/bbmp-tunnel-road-dpr-out-in-just-3-months-3263568 [SNIP]; ConstructionWorld 8 Jan 2025 [REC]

**Payments**
- "BBMP has already spent over Rs 140 million for DPRs, including **Rs 95 million to Rodic Consultants**" for the 18-km tunnel DPR. — ConstructionWorld, 21 May 2025 (citing The Indian Express), https://www.constructionworld.in/policy-updates-and-economic-news/bbmp-to-spend-rs-537-million-on-consultants-for-infra-projects/73823 [REC]. **[INF]** Taken at face value, this means essentially the full fee had been paid by May 2025, after the expert committee's report of 5 May 2025. No payment voucher, legislature reply or RTI record was found to confirm it.

**Penalties: two separate ones**
1. **Rs 5 lakh, for the Malegaon/Nashik error.** Traffic text for the cordon points (Malur Road, Guttahalli Main Road, NR Road) described "Malegaon–Nashik City / Nashik City–Malegaon" traffic. BBMP said the error was not in the draft DPR but crept into the executive summary that Rodic compiled. Engineer-in-Chief B S Prahallad said the consultants "acknowledged the mistake and apologised". Chief Commissioner Tushar Girinath rejected the "copy-paste" charge as limited to the summary and blamed a "compilation mistake by a junior officer". — ConstructionWorld, 10 Jan 2025, https://www.constructionworld.in/transport-infrastructure/highways-and-roads-infrastructure/bbmp-fines-consultants-for-nashik-malegaon-mix-up-in-tunnel-road-dpr/67487 [REC]; ConstructionWorld, 13 Jan 2025, .../bbmp-faces-backlash-over-errors-in-tunnel-road-draft-dpr/67527 [REC]
2. **Rs 10 lakh, for missing the deadline on the revised DPR** (the 16.6-km version). BBMP gave five working days to resubmit and warned of a 10% penalty if Rodic missed that too. — DH, https://www.deccanherald.com/india/karnataka/bengaluru/bbmp-slaps-rs-10-lakh-penalty-on-tunnel-road-consultant-3389674 [SNIP]; the date is not visible, roughly Jan–Feb 2025 **[INF]** from the article ID and the "February 3" deadline in the snippet. **No record found of whether either penalty was actually recovered, or whether the 10% penalty was triggered.**

**Expert committee (121 shortcomings)**
- The Urban Development Department set up the committee in April 2025 after the "copy-paste" criticism. It was chaired by Siddanagouda Hegaraddi, ED (Civil), BMRCL, and its report is dated **05.05.2025**. Its 89-page report spends about 50 pages on **121 shortcomings**. Among them: only 4 soil test points ("inadequate and unreliable"); no primary traffic surveys, only secondary data; no land acquisition plan, tree enumeration, EIA/SIA or utility plan (9 "important elements" missing); a shaft inside Lalbagh; the alignment almost parallel to the metro and no allowance for traffic diverting to it; a DPR commissioned before BMLTA review. Rodic submitted a revised DPR about two weeks later, saying it had addressed every observation; the committee remained unconvinced. — ConstructionWorld, 17 Oct 2025, https://www.constructionworld.in/transport-infrastructure/highways-and-roads-infrastructure/expert-panel-flags-major-flaws-in-bengaluru-tunnel-road-dpr/80249 [REC]; DH "Subterranean shock…", https://www.deccanherald.com/amp/story/india%2Fkarnataka%2Fbengaluru%2Fsubterranean-shock-bengaluru-s-tunnel-project-defies-due-process-3791668 [SNIP]; committee PDF on OpenCity (Cloudflare; not opened), https://data.opencity.in/dataset/35996dfd-6dd9-4eac-8b53-caec0ae82878/resource/2351a5e8-5f08-41b7-b53e-22b08a4092fa/download/hegaraddi-committee-main-report.pdf
- **Was the fee cut? No record found** of any cut, withholding or recovery linked to the 121 findings. The only money actions on record are the two penalties above (about Rs 15 lakh in all, if both stand, about 1.6% of Rs 9.45 cr **[INF]**). The May 2025 report says Rs 9.5 cr had been paid.

## 3. Fee norms and comparables

**Published norms found (weak; no single national ceiling)**
- MoRTH concept note on the DPR RFP (2025): for **standalone tunnel projects the fee is a "fixed cost assessed by Tender Inviting Agency"**, and bidders quote only a performance guarantee. For highways above Rs 100 cr the agency also fixes the cost. **There is no percentage formula** in the note. — https://morth.nic.in/sites/default/files/circulars_document/1-SR-Invitation of Public comments on DPR RFP concept Note.pdf [SNIP; the PDF did not render]
- Nagaland PWD notification compendium: a **2% of project cost** consultancy charge when its design cell prepares DPRs for non-state projects. — https://npwd.nagaland.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Compendium-of-Govt.-Notification-regarding-PWD-PD-_compressed.pdf [SNIP]
- A MoRTH/infracon consultancy evaluation document scores a fee around "2% of Estimated Project Cost" [SNIP; the PDF failed on TLS]. A trade report says DPR cost is "about 1%" of project value [SNIP; unverified, page 403].
- BBMP's own yardstick: an estimate of Rs 15 cr = "0.1%" of project cost (NewsKarnataka, 4 Sep 2024 [REC]).
- **[INF]** The 0.5–2% range in the brief is not confirmed as a binding norm. The sources reached support "1–2%" only as state-level or scoring conventions, mostly for highway DPRs of smaller value. Percentage rules break down for projects worth tens of thousands of crores, which is why MoRTH fixes tunnel fees instead.

**Arithmetic for Rodic's fee**
- 9.45 / 16,500 = **0.057%** (the brief's base). 9.45 / 17,698 (tender estimate, Jul 2025) = 0.053%. 9.45 / 19,000 = 0.050%. Against BBMP's 0.1% yardstick the fee is about 57–63% of its own estimate.
- Per km: 9.45 / 18 km = **Rs 0.53 cr/km**; 9.45 / 16.75 km = Rs 0.56 cr/km.

**The metro DPR (Sarjapur–Hebbal, Phase 3A), like for like**
- Consultant: RINA Consulting SpA, awarded about 30 Jul 2022 out of **10 bidders** (themetrorailguy, 17 Jun and 30 Jul 2022 [REC]). Its fee is reported as Rs 1.56 or 1.58 cr (NewsKarnataka, 4 Sep 2024, citing both figures [REC]) or as **Rs 10.5 million = Rs 1.05 cr** (ConstructionWorld, 8 Jan 2025 [REC]). No primary award notice was seen.
- Scope: **37.8 km, 23.5 km elevated (17 stations) + 14.3 km underground (10 stations)**; cost Rs 28,405 cr, revised to Rs 25,999 cr (Feb 2026 DPR) [SNIP]. It took about a year.
- Ratios: 9.45 / 1.56 = **6.1x** in total. Per km, 0.525 / (1.56/37.8 = 0.041) = **about 12.7x**. As a share of project cost the metro DPR was 1.56 / 28,405 = **0.0055%**, so Rodic's share is about 10x the metro's.
- **[INF]** The comparison is only partly like for like. The metro DPR is mostly elevated (only 14.3 km underground) and could draw on the BMRCL corridor work already done. A road-tunnel DPR needs ventilation, fire-life-safety, toll and ramp design. On the other hand, Rodic's DPR was delivered in 3 months with 4 bore points and no primary traffic survey (expert committee), i.e. a thinner scope than the fee implies. The metro fee itself looks unusually low: 10 bidders competed against Rodic's one.

**Verdict [INF]:** As a percentage of project cost, Rs 9.45 cr (0.05–0.06%) is **low** against every percentage yardstick found (BBMP's 0.1%, the 1–2% conventions). Per km it sits **in the band** of other urban tunnel DPRs (Kozhikode about 0.57, Rodic about 0.53, Worli–BKC about 1.06 cr/km). Against the BMRCL metro DPR it is **6x in total and about 12x per km**, but that metro fee looks like the outlier. The stronger public-interest points are not the headline fee: the **single-bid award** (with the only rival disqualified for a conflict), the **3-month delivery with minimal investigation**, **121 shortcomings with no fee cut found**, and **near-full payment reported by May 2025**.

## 6. Responses

- **Rodic:** no direct public statement found. On record only through others: it "acknowledged the mistake and apologised" (BBMP E-in-C B S Prahallad, Jan 2025), and it submitted a revised DPR saying it had addressed all of the committee's observations (DH [SNIP]). Nothing found in reply to Surya's allegations.
- **BBMP:** Chief Commissioner Tushar Girinath (Sept 2024) said few consultants bid and the quote was below the Rs 15 cr estimate; the project is complex and a first for the city. In Jan 2025 he denied a "copy-paste" job and blamed a junior officer's compilation error in the summary. BBMP imposed the Rs 5 lakh and Rs 10 lakh penalties.
- **B-SMILE:** its Technical Director dismissed the criticism, saying the city needs a road-based solution and that metro tunnelling shows tunnelling is feasible (DH [SNIP]). No B-SMILE statement on the DPR fee or quality was found.
- **State government:** it set up the Hegaraddi committee (Apr 2025); no final decision on the committee's report was reported. D K Shivakumar (then Deputy CM; later reported as CM in 2026 coverage) said only R Ashoka and Surya oppose the project, and "I am not a technical expert… He is also not an expert" (2025). In court the Advocate General opposed the PILs (Jul 2026). The state rejected Surya's kickback accusation (ConstructionWorld, 6 Jul 2026 [REC]). **No government response to the specific J&K, MP or BMRCL-copying allegations was found.**
- **Legislature:** no Assembly or Council reply on the DPR fee, payments or penalties was found.
