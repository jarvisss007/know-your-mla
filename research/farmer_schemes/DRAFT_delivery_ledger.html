Farm money: what was promised or sought, and what reached the field

DRAFT · NOT FOR PUBLICATION

Nine places where Karnataka's farm money can be followed from the announcement to the farmer, from audit reports, Parliament and Assembly replies, and the press. Each entry names the government that held the money at the time. Several gaps sit between the Centre and the state, and several run across governments of both parties.
